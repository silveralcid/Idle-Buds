# 5\. Presentation and content

World premise (owner approved): The Expanding Frontier. Years ago, mysterious portals appeared in the modern world, revealing new environments, resources and creatures called Buds. Humanity has adapted to them, developing established professions, industries and commerce, while new portals continue to open. Civilization is near-future with hints of magitech. Buds are predominantly benevolent or tamable; most exhibit animal-like intelligence, but some have extraordinary, unexplained intelligence and capabilities. Ordinary animals remain a proposed background-lore distinction, and uncanny similarities between some Buds and Earth animals offer potential intrigue without requiring an explanation or separate animal gameplay. Buds originated beyond the portals and have since established populations on Earth; the ultimate origins of Buds and portals remain mysterious. Owner-approved world/environment identity package (November 25, 2026): B/C/C/C/C/C. Earth is an established near-future civilization integrating portal technology, not a recent-discovery or post-apocalyptic setting. Portal worlds combine recognizable natural biomes with genuinely alien environments. New worlds introduce higher difficulty ranges, while earlier worlds retain advanced activities and long-term economic value. An original signature physical material interacts with or stores portal energy, with raw and refined forms; it is available early and connects technology, capture supplies and equipment. Present story through environmental lore, optional research entries and lightweight progression objectives, without mandatory dialogue or a structured campaign. Owner approved initial environment package A/C/C/C/C/C: Earth (familiar forests, grasslands, waterways and settlements), Living Frontier (extraordinary vegetation, bioluminescent ecosystems and organic resources), and Crystal Frontier (crystalline terrain, unusual geology and concentrated portal energy). The latter two are working names, not final branding. Use scientific designations alongside common world names. Most gathering skills span multiple worlds, with environment-specific strengths and resources. Bud affinities are influenced by ecology but not geographically restricted. Portal worlds combine research outposts with unexplored wilderness. Resonite is the working name for the signature portal-energy material. MVP content catalog, owner-approved B/B/C: target 6–9 distinct resources per gathering skill across the three initial tiers (not per tier); multiple equipment choices per tier rather than extensive upgrade trees; standard encounters, elite encounters and zone bosses using the existing combat system. Subsequent content-distribution batch C/C/C approved: overlapping resource tiers across worlds, with higher-tier resources more prevalent later; production recipe counts vary by the actual needs of each skill; Bud appearance and mechanical identity are developed together. These are planning targets, not final item counts or a binding release specification. Exact nodes, species assignments, designations, material acquisition and recipes, encounter rules, lore entries and progression objectives remain open. Owner raised concern about diminishing returns from excessive conceptual planning; recommendation is to stop further granular interviews and defer play-dependent choices until the implementation gate is lifted. Any final release specification and authorization remain separate owner decisions. No new research skill, quest system, implementation or release scope approved. Players are newly registered independent Tamers authorized to explore portals, gather and tame, not chosen heroes. The dominant tone is adventurous optimism, with cozy everyday life and an undercurrent of mystery. Keep narrative lightweight and avoid a mandatory campaign or apocalyptic premise. Owner-approved technology direction (November 25, 2026): Portal-powered retro-future with subtle magitech. Recognizable modern civilization incorporates portal materials and Bud capabilities into technology and industry; conventional resources and familiar everyday life coexist with extraordinary energy, materials and devices. Science and technology are the dominant framing, with mysterious phenomena and hints of magitech rather than a fundamentally magical civilization. Ordinary-to-extraordinary materials are a progression direction, not a fixed resource ladder. Exact resource names, energy/capture mechanics, production recipes and final visual treatment remain open. The consolidated production families Crafting and Synthesis are approved as part of the seven foundational noncombat skills. Owner-approved resource geography (November 25, 2026): Integrated Frontier (Option C), with a slight portal-first emphasis (B). Earth and portal environments both support useful resource gathering. Portals are the principal source of adventure, discovery and increasingly unusual environments, materials and Buds, while ordinary Earth resources should remain economically relevant throughout progression. Exact gathering locations, resource distribution, biome progression and release content remain open. Owner terminology decision (November 25, 2026): use Tender (plural Tenders) as the current working name for the player-character profession, replacing Trainer/Tamer in new design discussion and future design revisions. This is provisional, not final naming or trademark clearance. Historical Trainer/Tamer references in this document and archived visual studies refer to the same role, not separate classes or systems.

Owner-approved MVP world and content structure (November 25, 2026): A / A / B / B / A / C. Q1: the starting zone is on Earth. Q2: the other two initial zones are distinct portal environments. Q3: initial playable MVP targets three resource tiers within the longer-term five-tier planning model; exact materials, tier requirements and later tier rollout remain open. Q4: the initial 15-species roster uses overlapping regional populations alongside regional exclusives, rather than exactly five mutually exclusive species per zone. Q5: zone access, once earned through an eligible alternative progression path (owner subsequently selected D, combat or production-oriented milestones), is permanently unlocked rather than subject to recurring access charges. Travel between unlocked zones is instant. Zone progression must not impose a mandatory world-to-region-to-location navigation hierarchy. Owner subsequently approved the Melvor-inspired skill-first navigation direction: select a skill, then its nodes/recipes, or Combat, then zone-grouped encounter locations. Zone/location is contextual metadata on gathering nodes; locked nodes show requirements, with no manual movement step. Exact layout, grouping, mockups and location content are not finalized. Retain distinct world identities, regional encounter pools and exclusive species; shared species can occur in more than one location. Advanced recipe acquisition combines skill unlocks, exploration, combat rewards and activity-based discovery (owner selected C); use existing systems, not a new mandatory exploration mechanic. Exact paths, discovery odds and content remain open. Q6: gathering locations are normally static and reliable, with occasional special encounters. The exact form, triggers, rarity, content and release acceptance of special encounters remain open; this does not approve a new event system, mandatory interruption, or rotating ordinary nodes. Preserve personal production nodes, mixed universal/zone-specific resources, early-material utility, location-based weighted Bud encounter pools, the four-affinity MVP target (five to six at official release; portal affinities approved), and idle-first play. Exact zone names, biomes, species identities, node and encounter lists, resource/recipe tables, and balancing remain unresolved. No implementation, art commission, or binding release scope authorized.

Desktop-first with strong mobile support, shared accounts and persistent progression. Mobile portrait, mobile landscape and controller-friendly navigation are foundational requirements. Use adaptive layouts, legible touch targets, predictable directional focus, visible selection/focus states and alternatives to hover-only or drag-only actions. Preserve dense and efficient desktop views without forcing them onto phones. Owner approved browser-first qualification, then later multi-platform expansion targeting Steam, Steam Deck, mobile and desktop alongside browser access. Responsive desktop/mobile browser and controller-friendly design remain foundational. Native packaging/distribution sequence and platform-specific qualification are deferred; no all-platform launch commitment.

Owner decision (November 25, 2026): Creature-art production feasibility and the visual possibilities of the monster system will guide the final art direction, UI, aesthetic and world theme. The Expanding Frontier is now the agreed narrative setting; final art style, UI treatment and visual expression of the world remain conditional on achievable creature quality, distinctiveness, cost and scalability. Original, readable GBA-era pixel art, including Pokémon Emerald as a quality and complexity reference, remains a candidate rather than a binding style. Melvor Idle, Idle Clans and Super Auto Pets remain UI and presentation references. A small roster should be distinctive visually and mechanically. Research and nonfunctional design studies only while the BEAT BAES implementation gate is active; no modular asset commissions, production pipeline or prototype is authorized. Player-directed creature creation is an unapproved idea in the Idea Incubator.

**Visual identity target (owner direction): Clean, functional, information-first interfaces in the tradition of Idle Clans and Melvor Idle, with an unmistakable, cohesive personality and visual strength comparable to Balatro's distinctiveness. Balatro is a benchmark for recognizable art direction, not a request to imitate its card-game aesthetic, effects or typography. Preserve readability and long-session comfort on desktop and mobile. Nostalgic transparent late-1990s and early-2000s technology reinterpreted with modern usability is a strong owner preference and informs the selected MVP direction. References include iMac G3, translucent Game Boy/N64 hardware, Zenless Zone Zero, Mega Man Battle Network, Nothing OS, Poolsuite, Cassette Beasts and In Other Waters. The in-world Tamer terminal is a promising concept, not a mandatory interface metaphor. Rounds 1–2 explored eight concepts through nonfunctional Combat and Activities/Production mockups, including portrait and landscape for G and H. Use H as the default for subsequent design refinement and responsive validation. Do not treat G as a second required MVP theme, skin or implementation. **Creature-art production research (preferred direction to evaluate, not approved production scope): establish an original human-commissioned roster and signature Buds in a consistent style, then evaluate reference-guided AI generation and, only if it offers measurable benefits, a custom style adapter such as a LoRA trained on authorized commissioned artwork. Generate candidate sprites offline, curate and refine them with human judgment, and ship fixed assets rather than generating creatures during gameplay. Retain simple palette variants and conventional authored art where they are better or cheaper. Benchmark consistency, distinctiveness, cleanup time, rights and total cost against fully commissioned and modular artwork. The earlier 30–50 sprite dataset and cost estimates are exploratory, not procurement commitments. Obtain explicit artist agreement covering commercial use, modification, model training and generated derivatives; verify model licensing, applicable distribution disclosures and ownership limitations. No commissions, training, asset pipelines or implementation are authorized until the BEAT BAES demo is officially released. This is a content-production strategy, not a commitment to procedural creature gameplay, fusion or a player-facing creator.**

**Content strategy: Start with the smallest thoughtful set that supports the complete gameplay ecosystem.** Expand after release using new creatures, zones, abilities, skills, events and other qualified ideas. Maintain gameplay depth and avoid making new content obsolete existing collections. No production asset commissions are authorized while the BEAT BAES gate is active.
