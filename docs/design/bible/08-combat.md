## Current owner decision: observable automatic battles (November 26, 2026)

The Experience MVP must show a Super Auto Pets-like battle unfolding: opposing frontlines, visible Attack/Health, attacks, triggered abilities, damage/healing, defeats and advancing reserves. The battle log is supplementary.

The authoritative server resolver remains the single source of results. Record immutable starting formations and structured ordered events before participation XP changes. The client projects that recording; it does not run another fight or grant rewards. Pause, step, playback speed, skip and replay are presentation controls. Patrols continue in the background whether watched, paused, or on another screen. Keep only the current recording and newest result; no replay backlog.

Use the same presentation for ranked history. Existing ranked snapshots can reconstruct their original starting lineup without recalculation; old PvE records without snapshots keep an explicitly identified log fallback. Preserve simultaneous frontline hits and the current combat/formation rules. Broad stat balancing and final commissioned creature art remain later work.

## Auto-battling

**Combat combines Super Auto Pets-inspired automatic resolution, readable stats, positioning, triggered abilities, synergies and counterplay with Palworld-inspired trainer participation. The trainer is a meaningful combatant, including before acquiring a first Bud. Idle Buds monsters are owned, trained and traded over time, not drafted afresh each battle. Do not import Super Auto Pets' exact rules or Palworld's manual action combat. Owner-approved provisional battle format (November 25, 2026): Option A, sequential frontline auto-combat. Begin design and eventual sandbox evaluation with opposing frontline combatants clashing automatically, defeated frontline units being replaced by the next combatant, and triggered abilities/synergies affecting the broader formation. Keep it readable and idle-first. Individual action/turn-order combat (Option B) remains an alternative to evaluate only if the baseline proves inadequate; concurrent formation combat with independent action cooldowns (Option C) was discussed as a higher-complexity, unselected alternative. Neither is an additional approved combat mode. Exact damage resolution, timing, targeting, detailed trainer rules and balance are open; maximum formation is five Buds plus one trainer; no implementation or functional experimentation before the BEAT BAES release gate. Owner-approved combat XP distribution (November 25, 2026): shared participation (Option A). Each Bud assigned to a battle formation earns its own combat experience regardless of whether it reaches the frontline, attacks or triggers an ability. The participating Tender likewise earns relevant personal combat progression, including when acting as commander without entering the frontline. Do not weight XP by direct contribution or require inefficient positioning to train weaker Buds. This is shared participation eligibility, not a requirement for equal numeric XP across different progression tracks. Preserve independent XP ownership for production. XP amounts, victory/defeat eligibility and scaling, and exact Tender combat disciplines remain undecided. Owner-approved combat recovery direction (November 25, 2026): ordinary repeatable PvE encounters are independent battles (Option A), with health resetting between encounters, automatic repeat battles where eligible, and optional consumable enhancements rather than mandatory routine healing upkeep. Losing naturally reduces successful encounter rewards and catching opportunities; no additional universal recovery penalty is approved. Existing offline limits and activity-specific inputs apply. For special multi-round bosses, raids or events, including possible cooperative encounters, the owner favors persistent health across rounds within the encounter (Option B) to create endurance and preparation challenges. This is an approved conditional design direction for such future content, not approval of raids, cooperative combat or additional MVP features. Reuse the same auto-battle rules; exact healing opportunities, defeated combatant handling, encounter failure, consumable rules, reward eligibility and special-content scope remain open. PvP recovery rules are not decided by this PvE choice. Owner-approved optional between-battle preparation (November 25, 2026): the same automatically resolved battle activity can either repeat unattended, subject to ordinary offline and eligibility limits, or pause between encounters so the player may review results and adjust the team before continuing. Manual preparation is not manual combat. Use the same combat rules and existing team-building mechanics in both engagement styles; reward genuine strategic improvement, not clicking more often or exclusive active-mode bonuses. Enemy previews, reporting/replays, resume rules and async PvP opponent visibility/matching remain open. The optional preparation direction does not approve a separate engine, competitive mode or additional release scope. Owner-approved provisional attack expression (November 25, 2026): Option B, universal standard frontline hit based directly on current Attack, with one selectable learned technique expressed as a concise additional attack effect through the shared triggered-ability rules. No separate move-power statistic, action-selection AI, mana, accuracy rolls, cooldowns or move rotations. Retain species compatibility, individual Bud training, Tender progression and earned technique unlocks. Preserve Option A (plain stat-based hit without learned technique effects) as the simplest initial post-gate sandbox baseline and fallback if B does not provide meaningful fun/customization for its added complexity. Option C (fully distinct active move replacement) is cataloged but not selected. Low, mentally tractable Attack/Health numbers are the preferred baseline; exact ranges, caps, rounding, growth formulas, available techniques and trigger/damage order remain open.**

**Two primary strategic directions are intended: trainer-focused teams, in which Buds, gear and other progression choices enhance the trainer as the main combat threat; and Bud-focused teams, in which trainer skills, gear and other choices enhance the creatures as the main combat threat. Hybrids are valid. These are flexible build strategies, not locked classes, separate modes or a commitment to additional subsystems. Both should be meaningfully viable. Trainer participation has a provisional baseline below; gear effects, stats and balance remain open; formation capacity is approved below. Owner direction (November 25, 2026): initially deferred combat skill structure; subsequently reopened conceptual design and approved independent combat skills, initially with a derived Combat Level. The owner subsequently removed the derived level; only independent combat skills remain. Exact mechanics and final skill validation remain subject to later bounded sandbox experimentation. Such experimentation is a future validation step only; no game code or functional combat sandbox is authorized before the BEAT BAES demo is officially public and playable. Trainer participation decision and retained alternatives (November 25, 2026): A — unified ordinary combatant, occupying a normal formation slot with freely selectable position and the same attack, health, defeat and ability-trigger rules as Buds. Lowest rules/UX/implementation overhead and natural solo combat; trainer positional choice adds depth, but risks weak trainer identity and reduced Bud slots. B — separate supporting combatant outside the ordinary Bud formation, with attacks, buffs or support abilities; stronger Tamer identity and creature focus, but may require distinct timing, targeting, defeat and solo-combat rules. Pure passive support could be simple but may weaken trainer-focused builds. C — Protected Commander, owner-approved provisional baseline, visually separate trainer protected from enemy targeting while allied Buds remain, then entering frontline combat when they are all defeated. Preferred baseline expression for eventual testing: reuse the same combatant and triggered-ability rules, with a fixed rear/commander position, no independent turn clock, and trainer effects triggered by existing battle events. A trainer with no Buds begins as a normal frontline combatant. Owner selected C for conceptual design and future sandbox testing. This is a design promotion, not release-scope or implementation approval. See Idea Incubator for qualification and test questions. A is mechanically simplest; C may offer stronger identity and comparable build depth for modest additional complexity, but loses free trainer positioning and needs safeguards against unavoidable damage, overpowered protected support, and confusing targeting. B's complexity depends on how distinct its rules are. Retain A and B as cataloged alternatives, with A the simpler fallback; revisit only if C proves inadequate. C is the current provisional participation baseline; specific triggers, hostile area-effect behavior, abilities and exact combat-skill mechanics are not locked. The option letters here refer to trainer participation, distinct from the battle-resolution alternatives. Owner-approved combat team capacity (November 25, 2026): fixed maximum formation capacity available from the beginning, with the same maximum number of Bud slots for all players. No progression-gated or purchased extra combat slots. Early teams are naturally smaller because new players must catch and develop their Buds; no artificial slot restrictions are needed for onboarding. This is separate from the approved hybrid production deployment capacity progression. Owner-approved maximum formation: five Buds plus one trainer (Protected Commander under the current provisional participation model), for six combatants total. Empty Bud slots are allowed; the trainer can fight alone. This is the selected design baseline, subject to later playtesting and explicit scope approval.**

**Owner-approved conceptual Tender combat progression (November 25, 2026): independently trained combat skills, inspired by RuneScape/Melvor Idle. The previously selected derived Combat Level has been removed by explicit subsequent owner agreement. The current skill lineup is Melee (close-range weapon mastery), Ranged (conventional projectile weapon mastery), Resonance (working name for portal-powered weapons, extraordinary energy and device mastery), and Vitality (health/survivability, developed naturally through combat participation rather than a separate mandatory health-training activity). Skills follow the approved level-99 skill cap. Owner provisionally approved a Melvor-inspired hybrid participation-based XP model: encounter participation and difficulty determine XP rather than personal damage dealt; the equipped primary discipline (Melee, Ranged or Resonance) trains, Vitality advances naturally through participation, and genuine equipped Resonance support alongside Melee or Ranged earns a reduced share of Resonance XP. Dedicated Resonance support trains Resonance and Vitality. Preserve shared participation XP for every assigned Bud, including Buds that never reach the frontline. Balance the discipline XP budget so hybrid loadouts do not inherently advance faster than focused ones. Exact XP shares, encounter scaling, victory/defeat handling, eligibility, pacing, formulas and numerical effects remain open for later balancing. No overall Combat Level, separate XP track or universal combat power score. Use relevant skill requirements for equipment, specific progression milestones for content access, encounter difficulty guidance for PvE, and team-aware matchmaking/rating for PvP. A specialized Tender need not maximize every offensive discipline to remain competitive. A simple total of all skills is a possible informational/prestige statistic only; no new system approved. Equipment and earned progression unlock and express combat styles; no fixed classes are implied. The owner explicitly clarified Resonance should combine the practical roles of magic/magery and prayer from Melvor: offensive energy-based combat plus supportive or enhancing applications. Examples such as portal-powered damage, healing, shielding and Bud-enhancing devices are candidate expressions, not approved individual abilities or a new resource system. Resonance is a provisional name, replacing the proposed Technology label; Intelligence and other names are historical alternatives. The owner said they mostly liked the preceding six-question Tender package, not that every exact equipment slot, stat/trigger or acquisition rule was selected; only the explicitly agreed hybrid progression and Resonance direction are promoted here. Preserve the provisional Protected Commander formation, one shared automatic battle engine, existing battle-event triggers and viable Tender-focused, Bud-focused and hybrid builds. No new mana/prayer meter, mandatory upkeep, extra action clock, separate combat mode, exact equipment/ability slots or final formula authorized. Validate role overlap, XP eligibility and balance during later post-gate experiments. Subsequent owner selected hybrid Resonance integration and provisionally approved participation-based skill XP; exact loadout and XP eligibility checks remain open.**

**Owner-approved Resonance integration (November 25, 2026): Option C, hybrid. Resonance may serve as the Tender's primary offensive combat style or provide equipped supportive/enhancing effects alongside Melee or Ranged. This combines magic/magery-like offense and prayer-like support within the existing portal-tech discipline. Support must involve meaningful opportunity costs rather than being compulsory on every competitive build. Use the same automatic battle rules; exact loadout, eligibility, equipment/ability slots, triggers, numerical XP allocation, resource costs and balance remain open. The hybrid participation-based XP direction is provisionally approved. No extra mana/prayer meter, separate combat engine or independent action timer is authorized.**

**Owner-approved conceptual Tender equipment-bound ability model (November 25, 2026): the owner selected weapon-bound active techniques and armor-bound passive abilities instead of a separate learned/equipped ability library. A weapon determines the primary combat discipline (Melee, Ranged or Resonance), contributes Attack, and provides its automatically triggered technique through the shared battle-event rules. Armor contributes Health and provides its passive ability. Relevant combat-skill progression contributes to Attack/equipment eligibility/effectiveness; Vitality contributes to Health. No exact stat formula, accessory slot, ability pool, trigger or equipment-slot count is approved. Preserve hybrid Resonance support alongside physical styles via meaningful loadout tradeoffs, with exact expression open. Buds retain their previously approved species-linked ability, learned technique and genetic identity; the Tender's distinctive identity comes from crafted gear and skill progression. The separate learned Tender passive/active loadout is superseded as the baseline and retained only as a historical alternative. No implementation or release-scope approval.**

**Owner-directed crafting refinement (November 25, 2026): the owner wants individually distinctive crafted weapons with bounded Attack rolls and a compatible family of possible active techniques of differing rarity, to give Crafting meaningful specialization and support player-market desirability. Example: a Tier 1 energy sword could roll Attack 3–6; this is illustrative, not a fixed stat range, probability distribution or weapon specification. Owner also wants an optional player-facing Crafted by maker tag for crafted equipment (Ultima Online inspiration). Preserve maker identity across resale when the maker opts in; exact tagging workflow, metadata and display remain open. This is a proposed narrow revision of the previous blanket fixed crafting quality rule; the owner's weapon variability direction is accepted for conceptual qualification, not a finalized formula or approval of variable quality on every item. Owner subsequently approved the companion design: armor rolls bounded Health and one compatible passive; ordinary resources, tools, capture items, consumables and other routine goods retain fixed/predictable outputs; do not add additional affix slots or a separate reroll/enchantment system. Rarity should denote scarcity/strategic identity, not automatic power superiority. Crafting mastery improving crafted outcomes is approved in principle, alongside advanced and discoverable recipes for different and better results. A higher likelihood of desirable rolls is the recommended approach; exact skill effects, rarity and stat distributions, recipe unlock/discovery rules, Bud-assisted production implications, acquisition and market balance remain open. Owner subsequently approved permanent combat equipment with no durability, repairs or forced degradation, matching permanent gathering tools. Observe actual equipment supply and demand before qualifying optional sinks. Voluntary salvage remains HOLD; any future durability revision requires explicit owner approval. No functional experimentation, asset production or implementation before BEAT BAES gate.**

**Crafting and economy refinement: support two markets for individually valuable items, genetically distinctive Buds and distinctive crafted equipment. Artisan identity should emerge from useful equipment, scarcity, compatible technique choices and persistent optional maker signatures, rather than a dedicated reputation mechanic. A low-stat weapon with a suitable technique can outperform a high-stat weapon in the right composition; rarity is not automatic superiority. Example only: Tier 1 Energy Sword, Melee, Attack 3–6; Common / Power Strike / 4 Attack; Rare / Counterstrike / 5 Attack; Exceptional / Rallying Slash / 6 Attack; optional Crafted by: FrontierSmith. No implied stat-to-rarity correlation or fixed abilities. Keep proposed equipment variability limited to stat plus compatible ability, with ordinary goods predictable. Armor variability is now owner-approved, mirroring weapon variability. Crafting mastery should improve outcomes and unlock access to advanced recipes, including discoverable recipes that produce different and better results; preserve strategically meaningful lower-tier equipment. Exact mastery curve, probability shifts, requirements, discovery methods and balance are open.**

**Combat equipment durability: owner-approved no durability (November 25, 2026). Weapons and armor remain permanently usable without repair or forced degradation. No durability preserves prized gear but can accumulate supply; repairable wear creates material demand but adds friction and does not itself remove gear; permanent degradation sustains replacement demand at the cost of destroying possessions. Monitor supply and demand; any future revision requires evidence, qualification and explicit owner approval. Prefer demand from mastery, recipes and build diversity; voluntary salvage and future equipment alternatives are candidates, not commitments. No numerical supply threshold or mandatory sink approved. Gathering tools remain permanent under the prior approval. Voluntary salvage of unwanted equipment is a separate HOLD candidate, not an approved sink. See Living Game Design & Research Log 12.31 and Idea Incubator for full example, rationale and open qualifications. Design direction is not release scope or implementation authorization.**

**Strategy over raw power. A cleverly constructed team of relatively ordinary monsters must have a realistic chance against a more expensive, exceptionally bred team. Training and breeding remain meaningful advantages but must not guarantee victory. Determine the smallest combat model that proves this through later design exercises and authorized playtesting. Maximum team size (five Buds plus one Tender) and core Attack/Health model are approved; exact formulas, abilities, triggers, matchups, battle duration and rewards remain open.**
