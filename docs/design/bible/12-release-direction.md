# 7\. First release direction: MVP, not prototype

**Owner decision: Deliver a minimum viable product with full breadth of the approved core experience, not an isolated gameplay prototype. Horizontal completeness, vertical restraint. Every defining pillar should be experienced, but no system needs its mature-game depth or final content scale. Owner reaffirmed November 25, 2026: meaningful product validation requires the full interconnected gameplay breadth to be playable, not judging the game's overall appeal from an isolated combat/progression prototype. This is a validation goal, not a requirement to implement all systems simultaneously. Build and check incremental integrated slices after the BEAT BAES gate, then evaluate the complete small ecosystem. Bounded subsystem experiments remain useful for mechanics and feasibility but cannot substitute for holistic player validation.**

Proposed MVP envelope (not yet the binding release specification): a small, thoughtful monster roster; accessible catching and collection; trainer skills and monster-assisted production; basic training and inheritance; simple auto-battle combat; a working individual-monster and resource market; asynchronous PvP; basic social/cooperation; offline progress; desktop/mobile-friendly access; no live monetization during closed testing/public beta or before official launch. Monetization may be introduced only after official launch; exact date and catalog not fixed. Owner-approved content targets: initial playable MVP 15 Bud species and three zones; first official release 30–50 Bud species; all seven foundational noncombat skills, four MVP affinities and five to six first-official-release affinities (portal classification approved; Core, Surge, Bloom, Echo working MVP set), early/midgame content, temporary assets for closed tests. Detailed skill design explicitly to be revisited. Full breadth remains required for holistic validation. Scope and acceptance remain separate; first-release target must be qualified against evidence and sustainable capacity rather than expanding the initial MVP. Initial world structure: Earth starting zone, two distinct portal environments, three initial resource tiers, overlapping species populations with regional exclusives, permanent earned access, static gathering with occasional special encounters. Exact content and acceptance remain open.

**Defer:** synchronous PvP, advanced guild content and raids, roguelite modes, fusion, complex genetics, housing, elaborate tournaments, native platform clients and other expansion ideas. Their existence in the vision or incubator does not approve them for this release. Do not enlarge the MVP in order to prepare for them.

## What the MVP must prove

Players can make meaningful routine progress within 5–10 minutes. The idle loop is enjoyable independently. Monsters are desirable and useful. Catching, breeding, production and trading reinforce each other. Simple auto-battles support meaningful team-building choices. Multiplayer offers tangible benefits without forcing playstyles. Players voluntarily return for their own goals. These are qualitative validation targets; measurable acceptance criteria and playtest thresholds belong in the later release specification.

Owner-approved initial MVP qualitative acceptance targets (November 25, 2026): earn the first Bud within approximately five minutes of first-session play, superseding the earlier 10–15-minute first-Bud target; the full first-session loop and meaningful offline setup remain a 10–15-minute target. The first catch should be earned and approachable; high reliability is recommended, but exact encounter and probability rules remain open. Meaningful daily progress within 5–10 minutes of interaction; 15 Bud species, three zones, three initial resource tiers; all seven approved noncombat skills form a functioning economy; multiple viable combat team compositions; meaningful breeding inheritance and specialization; working player market with resource and individual Bud trading; chat, friends, basic clans and asynchronous PvP; reliable offline progress up to the approved 24-hour cap; playable desktop and mobile browser layouts with controller-friendly navigation. These are approved qualitative acceptance targets, not measured results or the final binding release specification. Exact test scenarios, numerical thresholds and balance remain open. Owner requested discussion of player-character/Tender combat next, reopening conceptual exploration while retaining the provisional Protected Commander role and deferring final mechanics to post-gate playtesting. No functional prototype or implementation before BEAT BAES demo release.
