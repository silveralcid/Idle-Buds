## Monster identity and individual value

**There is no single universally best monster.** Monsters can be exceptional in combat, gathering, crafting, adventure utility, breeding or collecting. Cute, cool, visually distinctive, unusually weak or otherwise rare specimens can have collector appeal even if they are poor combatants. Rarity creates an opportunity for demand, not a guaranteed price.

**Each Bud should have a reason to exist. Prefer a smaller, thoughtfully differentiated roster over a large roster of interchangeable sprites. Owner explicitly approved no evolution (Option A): every species is independent; progression comes from training, genetics, learned techniques, inherited traits and breeding. Preserve the approved planning targets of 15 distinct species for initial playable MVP and 30–50 for first official release; no evolutionary families or stages are required. Exact species catalog, trait lists and artwork remain open. Evolution remains a possible future idea only if explicitly qualified and approved. Owner-approved initial roster design (November 25, 2026), B/C/A/C/A/C: explicit common, uncommon, rare and exceptionally rare species categories (scarcity, not automatic power superiority); a mix of combat/production specialists and versatile generalists; approximately balanced representation across the four approved starting types; later species introduce distinctive and specialized strengths without making earlier species obsolete; fixed species work aptitudes, with individual level, genetics and traits improving performance instead of upgrading or learning base aptitudes; a signature ability for each species, alongside shareable learned techniques and inherited traits. Exact rarity rates, power ranges, roster allocation, signatures and inheritance rules remain open. Owner approved planning targets of four single-type affinities for MVP and five to six for the first official release, with approximately balanced representation and enough species to sustain coherent team synergies. Owner selected Option C, portal-phenomenon affinities, and endorsed the proposed stylized direction. Working MVP set: Core (matter/stability), Surge (energy), Bloom (life/adaptation), Echo (information/synchronization). Rift (space) and Null (entropy) are preferred candidate additions for the first official release, with five to six total types targeted. Replace the prior Earth/Wind/Fire/Water taxonomy. Affinities describe interaction with portal phenomena, not anatomy, origin or mandatory combat roles. Exact individual mechanics, names as final branding, symbols, colors, species assignment, and whether one or both additional types ship remain open. No universal damage chart, dual typing, binding release expansion or implementation approved.**
