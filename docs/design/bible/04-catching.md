## Catching and discovery

The first Bud must feel earned rather than handed out automatically. Early players learn to gather, craft and progress through their character, then experience a noticeable payoff when they catch and deploy their first creature. This must be approachable and reliable: the first earned Bud is targeted within approximately five minutes, with the complete first-session target of 10–15 minutes including basic understanding and offline setup. First catch uses a hybrid model: the player chooses where to hunt, encounters a Bud from a small eligible species pool and discovers randomized individual qualities. Every possible first Bud must be genuinely useful; avoid incentives to restart accounts for a better first roll. Wild catching requires combat, drawing on Palworld's acquisition loop. The trainer can fight without owning a Bud, so earning the first Bud does not depend on an existing collection. Breeding and trading provide alternative acquisition paths without personally hunting wild Buds. The exact starter species pool, onboarding encounter details, first-catch reliability and capture-probability values remain open; the post-victory capture rules are approved below. Owner-approved acquisition direction (November 25, 2026): preserve individually owned Bud specimens with persistent individual qualities and player-to-player trade regardless of thematic acquisition method; occasional wild eggs as a secondary loot/acquisition path, not a replacement for direct wild acquisition; player-crafted and tradeable capture consumables (balls or an equivalent portal-tech device) to support recurring material consumption, hunter/crafter interdependence, and viable specialization in manufacturing capture supplies within the existing Crafting skill. No additional profession XP track is approved. Owner approved automatic capture filters and capture-item selection inspired by PokéClicker (https\://github.com/pokeclicker/pokeclicker). Prefer legible prioritized condition-to-item-or-skip rules, with optional manual adjustments in active preparation and equivalent offline eligibility; exact rule criteria, tie-breaking, fallback on exhausted supplies and probability remain open. Owner explicitly approved consuming one capture item for each actual capture attempt, including failures; skipped candidates do not constitute an attempt. The owner is considering portal-tech bonding versus capture balls; neither exact physical design nor lore is locked; use generic capture-item terminology for mechanics planning. Owner-approved direct wild capture limit (November 25, 2026): after a victorious PvE encounter, at most one eligible encountered wild Bud may be selected and at most one capture attempt may be made, regardless of enemy formation size. A successful attempt acquires that individual Bud; failure consumes the selected capture item without acquiring a Bud. No retry or second target within that encounter. Skipping remains possible. Automatic filters determine the eligible target and item; exact tie-breaking, manual selection timing, unavailable-item behavior and success probability remain open. The limit applies to direct capture, not separately earned wild egg loot. Device lore, name and physical form explicitly deferred; use generic capture-item terminology. Aethermancer, Siralim Ultimate and Cassette Beasts are additional thematic references, not approvals of species-only unlocks or copying/reconstituting creatures. See Research Log Section 12.15 for source comparison and pinned PokéClicker source references. Owner-approved capture item progression (November 25, 2026): Option A, Palworld-inspired linear tiers of craftable and tradeable capture consumables. Higher tiers increase capture effectiveness and generally require more costly materials or crafting progression. Lower tiers should remain economically useful for easier catches even for experienced Tenders. Automatic filters can select appropriate tiers. No fixed tier count, recipes, item names, formula, prices or numerical probabilities are approved. Specialized items (type-, rarity- or circumstance-specific) and the hybrid tier-plus-specialization model remain optional future candidates, not approved requirements. Owner-approved capture probability factors (November 25, 2026): use species-defined natural capture difficulty, selected capture item effectiveness, and Tender progression modifiers, broadly inspired by Pokémon and Palworld while adapting to the post-victory one-attempt system. No separate Catching skill; exact Tender progression source, interaction between factors, numerical probabilities, floors/caps, and item-tier scaling remain open. Do not require remaining enemy HP or combat-performance scoring in the initial model. Whether individual rarity/traits affect capture difficulty independently remains an open design question, not an approved modifier. Preserve the usefulness of economical low-tier capture items.

**Predictable rules, unpredictable discoveries. Players should be able to deliberately hunt species through consistent locations or encounter rules, while unexpected individual qualities and rare discoveries keep each catch exciting. Deliberate hunting and surprise are equally important. Authored discoveries and emergent variation can coexist. No particular biome, encounter table, IV/EV system, nature or mutation mechanic is yet approved. Owner-approved encounter selection (November 25, 2026): Option A, location-based hunting with weighted wild-Bud encounter pools. Players choose where to hunt, not a guaranteed species; consistent location pools support deliberate pursuit and unexpected discoveries. Exact encounter compositions, weights, location unlocks and presentation remain open. Species-targeted encounter boosters (Option C) are retained as a possible future expansion in the Idea Incubator, not approved core mechanics; direct guaranteed species selection (Option B) not selected. Owner explicitly deferred rare visual variants (November 25, 2026): shinies, alternate palettes, special appearances and similar collector variants remain future candidates in the Idea Incubator, not foundational collection requirements. Individual genetic potential is now owner-approved: Attack, Health, Gather and Craft IVs plus inherited traits, adapted from Palworld-style Talent without importing its exact numerical or breeding rules. Exact IV values/distributions and trait generation remain open.**

**Owner-approved collection management (November 25, 2026): players can trade/sell individually owned Buds through player-to-player commerce, release unwanted Buds permanently, and use a Palworld-inspired condensation system in which eligible duplicate Buds are consumed to improve another Bud. These three disposition paths are approved design directions; a separate recycling/exchange-for-materials system was discussed but not selected. Condensation should support demand for otherwise ordinary specimens and be meaningful without becoming an excessive mandatory grind. Exact duplicate eligibility, benefits, input counts, costs, tiers, caps and interaction with genetics/aptitudes remain open. Do not assume a specific Palworld condensation formula. Releasing removes a Bud from circulation; no particular compensation or currency faucet is approved. Fast, convenient collection management is an explicit owner requirement: provide useful inherent quality-of-life and some additional earnable conveniences, with low-friction handling of large collections and unwanted specimens consistent with idle-first play. Specific features are not yet approved; candidate examples include search/sort/filter, multi-select and bulk actions, favorites/protection against accidental disposition, comparison views and earned presets or automation. Basic safety and usability should not require a paid or late-game upgrade. Storage capacity, listing workflow, overflow handling, advanced automation and earned unlock requirements remain open. Egg moves (breeding-specific learned technique access) and additional specialized passive variants are owner-requested future ideas in the Incubator, not approved expansion mechanics. Preserve existing inherited-trait system and four IVs; no extra slots or effect systems implied.**
