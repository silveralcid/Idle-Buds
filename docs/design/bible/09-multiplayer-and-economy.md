# 4\. Multiplayer, economy and competitive play

## Open sandbox, meaningful interdependence

**No locked classes or professions.** Players can eventually master all skills and participate in any gameplay path. Specialization emerges from how players invest their time, resources, knowledge and attention. A dedicated collector, producer, breeder, merchant or battler can independently be a successful player.

Self-sufficiency should be possible, but working with other players and specializing should generally be more efficient. Most meaningful progression and acquisitions should be accessible alone, potentially taking longer. The owner's 80/20 principle favors broad solo access with clear incentives and some distinctive cooperative rewards; this is not an exact content quota.

Multiplayer onboarding (owner approved): introduce community and market visibility immediately, with actual participation opening naturally through early progression. Chat, rankings and market browsing are intended to show the living world from the start; resource trading and clan participation should become accessible early. Protect the earned-first-Bud experience before opening Bud trading, and introduce PvP after basic combat is understood. Exact unlock conditions and timing are not yet fixed. Trading is the most fundamental form of asynchronous cooperation. Social and group features should encourage players to help one another without turning regular play into mandatory attendance. Basic social/cooperative functionality belongs in the proposed MVP envelope; specific guild systems, shared challenges and raids are not yet release-approved. Owner-approved social/cooperation foundation (November 25, 2026): Q1 A+C, interpreted as C inclusive of A: global chat, friends and clans. Q2 B initially, shared clan progression and bonuses; competitive clan wars (C) deferred. Q3 A initially, asynchronous shared contribution goals; live group battles (B) deferred. Preserve optional participation, idle-first cooperation and meaningful value for solo players. Exact clan size, progression, bonus scope, shared goal rules, chat/friend controls, moderation and contribution rewards remain open. Advanced clan/co-op systems are future candidates, not release commitments.

## Marketplace

**Open trading by default. Player-to-player trade is the primary economy, and ordinary market prices should be driven by players. Account-bound exceptions require a compelling reason, such as preserving earned competitive recognition or preventing economic abuse. Owner-approved marketplace foundation (November 25, 2026): Q1 A, one global shared marketplace; Q2 C, both sell listings and player-funded buy orders; Q3 C, hybrid stock-based NPC general store as a limited fallback for eligible goods; Q4 A, no NPC buying or selling Buds; Q5 C, successful-sale tax as a currency sink, not a fee for unsuccessful listings; Q6 A, fixed-price Bud listings initially rather than auctions. Exact buy-order matching, item categories, tax rates, settlement and listing details remain open. Individual Bud market remains player-driven. Auctions are a future alternative, not approved baseline scope. This supersedes the earlier player-only goods-market proposal and pending NPC store status. No implementation or release scope approved.**

**Low-volume resilience: essential progression must remain possible through ordinary self-sufficient gameplay when listings are unavailable. A unified global player market and player-funded buy orders are approved. Trading should accelerate progression without making basic progression depend on other players being online or listing necessities. The approved stock-based NPC store is a fallback, not the primary market.**

**OWNER-APPROVED ECONOMY DIRECTION (November 25, 2026): Hybrid NPC general store. The owner proposed an NPC that always buys eligible goods at a simple baseline value and sells only what is in stock, combining minimum/maximum inventory targets with stock contributed by players. The aim is a reliable fallback during low trading volume, not a second economy that displaces player trade. Approved baseline direction: one general store, conservative fixed buyback prices, higher fixed selling prices, bounded stock, limited gradual restocking of essential starter materials, and advanced goods supplied primarily by player sales. Excess inventory removal is a candidate item sink; exact stock controls remain open. Example only: NPC buys copper at 2 gold and sells it at 10; player trading at 5 benefits both sides. Specific prices, eligible goods, stock limits, restocking rates and overflow behavior remain open. Buds are excluded from NPC buying and selling.**

**Risks and unresolved decisions: unlimited buyback creates a currency faucet even if inventory is capped; low prices alone may not prevent gold farming or inflation. Stock floors, item sinks, currency sinks and possible safeguards require economy design and testing. Owner approved no NPC Bud trading; excluding other rare collectibles and premium items remains a recommended candidate restriction, not a blanket owner-approved category rule. No dynamic pricing algorithms, simulated merchants or additional market-making systems are assumed. Tax rates, NPC buyback and replenishment controls, anti-abuse safeguards and precise eligible goods remain open. The approved store and its still-open guardrails are recorded in the Idea Incubator.**

Purchasing a powerful monster must not completely bypass trainer progression: the trainer's own mastery determines how effectively the purchased creature can be used. Exact rules and thresholds remain undecided. Ensure prices can reflect utility, rarity and player demand without allowing uncontrolled breeding supply or paid acceleration to undermine the market.

## PvP

**PvP is optional, prestigious and easy to try. It is a core product pillar but not the only endgame or a requirement for normal progression. Asynchronous battles are the first-release competitive direction. Synchronous auto-battles remain part of the long-term vision, with format and timing deferred. Do not require players to be online at particular times for ordinary competitive participation. Owner-approved ranked foundation: Q4 A, blind asynchronous ranked matchmaking rather than direct opponent selection for ranking; additional future formats retained. Q5 A, actual Bud stats initially to preserve training/breeding value, with strong/fair matchmaking explicitly requested; future C (separate formats, including possible normalized formats) retained. Exact rating system, matching signals, power bands, defense snapshots, counter-picking safeguards, PvP recovery and abuse controls remain open. No specific normalization or live competitive implementation approved.**

**Owner-approved PvP reward direction (Q6 A): prestige and cosmetics, not exclusive functional power. Examples: titles, special cosmetic items and distinctive visual variants. Prestige must be earned, not purchased as a substitute for competitive achievement. Cosmetic variants should remain distinguishable from gameplay-relevant rare specimens where necessary. Exact reward catalog, rank/season structure and matchmaking mechanics remain open.**
