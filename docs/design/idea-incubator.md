# Idle Buds Idea Incubator

## Working title \| Ideas → Qualification → Promotion

This is the safe place for every exciting idea, regardless of cost, complexity or likelihood of shipping. Nothing in this document is a requirement, a promise, a release blocker or permission to implement. Do not add new speculative features directly to the Game Design Bible or release specification.

Release gate: BEAT BAES must be officially released as a public demo before any Idle Buds implementation. Research, planning and design are allowed now. No functional prototypes, code or production asset commissions.

# 1\. Ideas \| Capture freely

Add ideas here as soon as they occur. A title and a sentence about why it sounds exciting are enough. Do not estimate, prioritize, score, architect or justify the idea during capture. Add more detail only if it helps preserve the thought.

Simple entry: \[Idea\] — \[What sounds fun or interesting?\]

# 2\. Qualification \| Revisit with a clear head

When an idea still appeals after the initial excitement fades, move it here for a brief review. No mandatory waiting period or review meeting; review when making design decisions. Avoid turning this into a second backlog management system.

Ask three questions:

1\. Fit: Which core player experience or actual problem does this serve? Would Idle Buds be meaningfully better, rather than merely larger?

2\. Simplicity: What is the smallest version that delivers the benefit? What can it reuse, and what ongoing content, balancing or maintenance burden does it introduce?

3\. Priority: Is there sufficient evidence or design reasoning to justify it over the alternatives? What must we postpone, replace or sacrifice to make room?

Record a one-line outcome: PROMOTE (clear fit and value), HOLD (promising but premature or uncertain), or DROP (not worth pursuing). A HOLD can return to the ideas section; a DROP can be kept here for reference or removed. A design-only test or competitor research can resolve uncertainty. No implementation experiments while the gate is active.

# Rule for future conversations

When we brainstorm a feature, capture it here by default. Do not modify approved design or release scope just because the discussion is exciting. Revisit and qualify ideas deliberately. Keep this document light, enjoyable and easy to use. The process is inspired by the idea-first approach the owner associates with Tynan Sylvester; this workflow is our adaptation, not a claim about his exact wording.
