# Idle Buds

## Game Design Bible v0.1 \| November 25, 2026

**STATUS: Foundational design decisions consolidated from the owner interview. Planning only. Idle Buds is a temporary working title. This is not a release specification, implementation plan, or authorization to develop the game. Current checkpoint: initial player journey, world/theme, skilling/production and combat fundamentals interviews complete. Tender is the provisional player-role name. Completed interview: catching and creature acquisition; capture filters, crafted tradeable supplies and secondary wild eggs approved, post-victory direct capture with one target and one attempt per encounter, consuming one item per attempt including failure, approved; device lore deferred. Location-based weighted encounter pools approved; targeted encounter boosters deferred. Linear capture-item tiers approved; specialized items deferred. Capture probability factors approved: species difficulty, capture-item effectiveness and Tender progression. Catching Q5 approved: four IVs (Attack, Health, Gather, Craft) plus inherited traits; visual variants deferred. Attack/Health remains the combat baseline; Defense retained only as a testing alternative. Catching Q6 approved: player trading/market sales, release, and condensation, with efficient inherent and earnable collection-management conveniences. Catching interview complete. Completed interview: breeding and inheritance. Q1 approved: same-species and compatible cross-species breeding, selectable primary parent determining offspring species, both parents contributing genetics, and offspring-species preview. Q2 approved: traditional male/female breeding for most Buds, with rare/unusual species-specific exceptions. Q3–Q6 approved: time-only baseline with optional enhancements (interpretation of A+C), independent IV inheritance with random-generation chance, parental trait inheritance with new-trait chance, and egg production followed by incubation. Breeding interview complete. Completed marketplace and economy interview: global shared player market with sell listings and buy orders; stock-based hybrid NPC general store for eligible goods; no NPC Bud trading; successful-sale tax; fixed-price Bud listings initially. Completed multiplayer and cooperation interview: global chat, friends and clans; shared clan progression/bonuses; shared contribution goals; blind async ranked matchmaking using actual Bud stats and good matchmaking; prestige/cosmetic PvP rewards. Future competitive clan wars, live group activities, additional PvP modes and separate normalized formats retained as deferred candidates. Completed monetization interview: free-to-play, primarily permanent upgrades with some consumable purchases, earnable/purchasable account-bound premium currency, gameplay-earned equivalents for paid acceleration plus cosmetics, permanent shop and occasional supporter bundles. Foundational design and release-direction interviews complete. Browser-first qualification, small complete ecosystem, closed testing then public beta then official launch, monetization only after official launch, quality-driven updates (consider scheduled cadence when commercial results justify it), integrated breadth-first validation. Approved browser-first React/TypeScript/Vite direction, initial TypeScript/Node/Fastify backend and PostgreSQL, Tauri preferred for later desktop qualification. MVP content boundaries approved: 15 species for the initial playable MVP, 30–50 target for first official release, three initial zones, seven foundational noncombat skills (revisit detailed design), four starting affinity types for MVP and five to six for first official release (portal affinities approved; working MVP names Core, Surge, Bloom, Echo; potential release additions Rift and Null), early and midgame content, temporary testing assets. Progression and balancing interview complete: Tender skills cap 99; hybrid XP curve; Bud level cap 99 (independent XP pacing); alternative combat or production-based permanent zone unlocking (owner subsequently selected D); no prestige resets; earlier content retains situational value. Seven noncombat skill responsibilities and shared skilling mechanics approved: five resource tiers, upgradeable Tender gathering tools, primary resources plus rare secondary drops, fixed quality baseline for routine crafting (weapon variability refinement proposed), level unlocks plus discoverable recipes, aptitude-based Bud work without individual tools. Resource and production economy approved: mixed availability, earlier material relevance, permanent tools, multi-recipe queues, upgradeable storage, mostly tradeable equipment with exceptions. MVP world/content structure approved: Earth starting zone and two distinct portal environments, three initial resource tiers, overlapping Bud populations with regional exclusives, permanent earned zone access, static gathering locations with occasional special encounters. Initial qualitative MVP acceptance targets approved, with first Bud within approximately five minutes and full first-session loop within 10–15 minutes. Conceptual Tender progression approved: individual level-99 Melee, Ranged, Resonance (working name, offensive and prayer-like support) and Vitality skills (derived Combat Level removed by subsequent owner decision), with independent XP pacing and equipment-driven specialization. Resonance hybrid integration approved (primary offense or supplemental support alongside physical styles). Tender weapon-bound active techniques and armor-bound passives approved. Owner proposed variable weapon stat/technique outcomes and requested optional crafted-by marks; armor variability and Crafting mastery with advanced/discoverable recipes now owner-approved; no combat gear durability now approved. Tender combat XP allocation provisionally owner-approved (participation and encounter difficulty, equipped disciplines plus Vitality); exact rates and detailed crafting/economy mechanics remain open. Derived Combat Level removed; owner reaffirmed level 99 for all Tender skills and individual Bud levels after comparing 99 versus 100; defer numerical formulas until balancing; final combat mechanics await post-gate sandbox validation. Exact release specification not yet approved. Full chronological decisions and alternatives are preserved in the Living Game Design & Research Log, Section 12\.**
