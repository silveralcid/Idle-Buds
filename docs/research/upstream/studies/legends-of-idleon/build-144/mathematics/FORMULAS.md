## Model results (`simulations/idleon_model.py`)
- **Every skill has its own ladder.** EXP from level 1 to 100: character 1.39·10⁹, default skill 2.8·10¹⁰, Construction 4.5·10⁷, Laboratory 7.2·10⁵, Divinity 6.0·10⁵, Sailing 4.7·10⁷, Breeding 1.9·10⁹, Gaming 2.3·10¹³, Research 3.5·10¹⁴. The geometric base shrinks with level and then holds (character ×1.044 per level from about L322; default skill ×1.09), so curves bend from exponential toward a fixed growth rate; Gaming (×1.13 twice above L20) and Research never level off.
- **One bonus language.** Talents use decay 197×, add 89×, bigBase 50×; bubbles decay 106×; vials add 85×; stamps add 90× / decay 38×. `decay` saturates at x1 (half at n = x2), so most bonuses are front-loaded and late levels are mostly about the long tail of sources rather than any one upgrade.
- **Accuracy is a hard gate.** Below half a monster's Defence the hit chance is 0 and AFK fighting yields nothing; at parity 52 %; at 1.5× it reaches 99–100 %. Because hit chance is counted twice in the AFK kill rate, AFK kills at 52 % hit chance are about half of what one application would give (Crabcake example: 41 vs 78 kills/h).
- **Defence is strong against weak hits only.** At 100 Defence a 100-damage hit does nothing and a 1 000-damage hit is cut to 451; at 500 Defence a 10 000 hit still lands for 1 463.
- **Damage soft caps** compress 10¹² of raw Multi into ≈2·10⁹ and raw Base of 10⁶ into ≈5·10⁴; growth stays possible but each order of magnitude costs several.
- **Drop odds** with expected counts at or below 2 per batch undercount: −10 % at an expected 1 drop, −27 % at 2; above 2 the result is the rounded mean with no variance. Large AFK batches (many kills at once) therefore get the mean; small batches of rare drops are shaved.
- **Stamps** cost coins geometrically (Sword Stamp: 14 coins at L1, 3 500 at L60, 1.7·10⁵ at L100) with material levels every 2–20 levels whose cost grows as exp^(k^0.8).
- **Skilling needs ten times the requirement** for a certain harvest (harvest chance 23 % at efficiency = node Defence, 50 % at 2.6×, 100 % from about 10×), so efficiency keeps paying long after a node is unlocked; AFK skilling yield scales with the same chance.
- **AFK bases**: fighting 40 %, skilling 52 % of the active rate before bonuses; offline time has no general cap in the code beyond the prayer curse; food and carry capacity limit what a long absence returns.
