## 5. Max damage = Base x Percent-multiplier x Multiplier, each soft-capped
raw:            1e+03     4e+03     1e+04     1e+05     1e+06     1e+09     1e+12
base cap:       1e+03     4e+03  6.74e+03  1.96e+04  5.22e+04  7.59e+06  1.49e+09
pct cap:          447  1.33e+03  2.83e+03     2e+04  1.45e+05  1.05e+07  1.24e+08
multi cap:        447  1.33e+03  2.83e+03     2e+04  1.45e+05  4.49e+07  2.02e+09
Base = ((WeaponPower*(1+talents)+t5)/3)^2 + primary stat + gold food + min(150, 2*WP + stat) + flat sources;
min damage = max * mastery, mastery = min(0.8, 0.35 + sources); expected hit = max*(mastery+(1-mastery)/2).
Note the order: the Percent factor soft-caps after its own multiplications, and about 20 further factors come after the Multiplier caps.
Expected hit / max at mastery 0.35: 0.675; at the 0.8 cap: 0.900

## 8. AFK gain rate bases
Fighting: 0.40 + sum(bonuses)/100, times two multipliers; Mining, Choppin, Fishing, Catching, Cooking, Laboratory:
0.50 + (2 + card + other "all" bonuses)/100 -> 52 % with nothing else; Divinity 1.00; Spelunking 0.50; Research 0.01; floor 1 %.
Bundle bun_u adds +30 points to the "all" pool (fighting 0.70, skills 0.82).
Away time = GlobalTime (server time at each sync via "ttg", advanced locally between syncs) - per-character Player timestamp;
gaps <= 120 s give nothing; gaps > 2e8 s or a stamp below 1e4 are reset;
the Unending Energy prayer caps it at 36 000 s (10 h). No other general cap is applied in AFKgains; account systems cap their own stores (anvil, forge, worship, Dream).
