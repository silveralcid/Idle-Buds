# Kadomon — Progression
In-run: EXP (60 / 180 thresholds) → 3 evolution stages (stats jump, super/passive magnitudes indexed by stage via `X/Y/Z[stage]`); types can be added at camps or by passives (`CanAddType`, up to the `bonusTypes` list); items (29, incl. 3 craftable recipes) and consumables (10) add `bonusStats`/start statuses; field effects (23) are run-wide modifiers chosen every 3 battles; morale 100 is the run's HP. Enemy strength is pegged to the player's accumulated EXP budget (+75 per battle) rather than to time, so a player who does not merge falls behind the curve by design.
Meta: Kadodex (catch/evolve unlocks), misprint skins saved at camps, per-unit battle stats, Steam achievements; a single region is playable ("prologue"), 12 area assets and 3 gym leaders exist in data for later regions (STRONG INFERENCE: content pipeline ahead of release).
