# Kadomon — Economy
Currencies: **Gold** (2 per battle + field effects + GAIN_GOLD effects; spent in shops 5/7/10 by rarity; binning an item returns 1, releasing a unit returns none), **Shop tickets** (1 at start, +1 per mini-boss/boss; 1 ticket restocks the shop), **EXP** (the real progression currency: battles +5, merges +30, release +10, skip +5, camp +30), **Morale** (100, −10 per surviving enemy on a loss). Premium: none; paid product (Steam) with no IAP code. Sinks are deliberately small so the free recruit-a-defeated-enemy stream dominates team building; gold mainly buys a specific missing evolution copy.
