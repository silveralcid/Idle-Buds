# Kadomon — UX and Design Psychology (static analysis of code/data; no play session)
- **Readable tempo**: SPEED is "ticks until attack" and the action-order strip (`ActionOrderDisplayer`) shows the queued actions, so the player can read who moves next — a Pokémon-flavoured auto-battler where the core skill is slot ordering (front/back, PIERCE/SNIPER/TAUNT geometry) and type pairing.
- **Pokémon affordances**: 11-type chart with only "super effective" (no resistances) keeps the chart learnable; dual types make almost every match-up have an edge; evolution at fixed EXP with stat jumps, misprints as shinies, a Kadodex, gym leaders.
- **Friction/risks found in code**: battles are unseeded, so a lost battle cannot be re-watched identically; enemy strength tracks the player's total EXP (a snowball guard that can punish non-merging players invisibly); the recruit-defeated-enemy reward means enemy pools define the meta per area; morale −10 per surviving enemy makes close losses cheap and wipes expensive (a comeback-friendly curve).
- **Retention hooks**: Kadodex completion, misprint hunting (camp-site saves), per-unit stats screen, achievements; only one region shipped in this build.
