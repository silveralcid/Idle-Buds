# Kadomon — Gameplay Systems (VERIFIED from source unless marked)

## Core loop
Roguelike run on a 12-layer procedural map (`MapConfig "FullWorld"`), template Battle, Battle, Mystery, Battle, Battle, Rest, Battle, Battle, Mystery, Battle, Rest, **Boss** (7 battle layers; each layer has a `randomizeNodes` chance of 0.09–0.8 to swap type), with 3 shops and 1 mini-boss inserted by the generator (`numOfShops 3`, `numOfMiniboss 1`, 4 starting nodes, 5 pre-boss nodes). Node types: MinorEnemy, EliteEnemy, RestSite, Treasure, Store, Boss, Mystery, Mini_Boss. Player **Morale** starts at 100; losing a battle costs 10 per surviving enemy (`damagePerEnemyAlive = 10`); 0 = run over. After every battle fought (win, or loss with morale left): +2 gold on the reward screen, +5 EXP to every owned Kadomon, +75 EXP to the enemy budget, and **recruit one of the enemies you fought** (pre-battle copies with their spawn EXP/item) or skip for +5 EXP to all; an item every 2 battles and a field-effect choice every 3 (`itemsEvery`, `fieldEvery`; the boss grants both at once); boss and mini-boss fights give +1 shop ticket instead of a unit pick.

## Team and stats
Party of up to 4 (`currentEnemies[4]`, `playerPartyUnits`) plus a box. Four stats: HP, ATTACK, **SPEED (ticks between attacks, lower = faster)**, **ENERGY (ticks to charge a SUPER)**. Stats come only from the current evolution stage (`UnitScriptable.GetStat = currentMon.stat + bonusStats`); there is no per-level stat growth outside evolving. Bounds in `UnitDictionary`: HP 5–120, ATK 4–21, SPD 8–21, NRG 8–30.

## Battle resolution (tick model)
Each tick (`BattleManager.OnTick` → `Entity.Tick` for every living unit, player slots first):
1. `AttackTick`: `currentTick++`; when `currentTick ≥ GetBattleSpeed` (base speed − FAST stacks + SLOW stacks, min 1) the unit performs its basic attack (`AttackScriptable.Tick`), then `currentTick −= speed` and one FAST/SLOW/HIDDEN stack is consumed.
2. `AddEnergy(1)`: when `currentEnergy ≥ GetBattleEnergy` (base energy + 2 × supers used if DRAINING) the unit fires its **SUPER** (`OnEnergyPassiveScriptable`), `supersUsed++`, energy −= required.
3. Targeting: basic attack hits the front enemy unless TAUNT / HIDDEN / keywords (SNIPER = back enemy, WILD = random, VOLLEY = all enemies, ASSASSIN = lowest max-HP enemy); enemy teams line up sorted by HP descending (with per-unit position overrides); 28 `TARGETING` modes exist for effects (lowest/highest HP, infront/behind, reinforcements, summons…).
4. Damage (`DamageHelper.DamageToTake`): `ceil(attack × Π type multipliers + FRAIL stacks + FATIGUE stacks)`, where attack = stage attack + ATTACK stacks (+ shield if SHIELD_BASH, + low-health bonus, − decay penalty) and the multiplier is **1.25 for every (attacker type, defender type) pair marked effective** (a dual-type attacker vs a dual-type defender can reach 1.25^4 ≈ 2.44); there is no resistance (`resistanceMulti = 1`). STRENGTH stacks are added flat after the formula on basic attacks only, then all consumed. Fixed-value effects (`attack.value > 0`, `dealExactDamage`) replace the formula (STRENGTH still adds). Field effects can reduce damage to the front ally (min 1). Every basic attack also costs the attacker 1 SHIELD and triggers DIZZY if present.
5. Mitigation order (`Entity.GetHit`): FORTITUDE blocks the hit entirely (1 stack); SOLID_ARMOUR reduces by `stacks %` (min 1) while shielded; `HealthController.GetHit` drains **SHIELD** first, remainder to HP. On 0 HP: LAST_WISH revives with `stacks` HP as a ghost and force-fires the super **once per stack**; otherwise death is queued as an action (DEATH speed) → `OnDeath`/`OnUnitDeath` passives, FRAGILE (player unit released: +10 EXP to all). `StatusEffectHelper.OnExplode` exists (stacks split over living enemies) but no caller was found in the source — EXPLODE may be dead code in this build (STRONG INFERENCE).
6. After a basic attack (`CheckPassives`): THORNS on the defender retaliates (1 damage per stack, consume 1), DECAY self-damages the attacker (stacks, consume 1), REGEN heals (stacks, then remove half), SPLASH hits all other enemies (stacks), PIERCE hits the unit behind the target for the same damage, BATTERY gains CHARGE, FRAIL is fully consumed, TAUNT loses 1.
7. Position: units slide forward to fill gaps (`CheckMoveForwards`); PUSH_BACK/MOVE_SELF effects reorder; summons (`SpawnToken`) insert front or back; bosses stay last.
8. **Fatigue clock**: once the round counter passes 150, every 10th round (160, 170, …) every unit gains 1 FATIGUE (+1 damage taken per hit per stack) → no infinite stalls.
9. Start of battle (tick 5): `StartPassives` — `OnSpawn` passives, `startEffects`, keyword starters (STEALTH = 5 HIDDEN, SHELTER = faint the ally ahead and store a copy, taunt supers).

## Status effects (28 `STATUS_EFFECT`, `StatusEffectScriptable` texts)
Asleep (skip next attack), Attack (+damage), Charge (resource), Decay (self-damage on attack), Dizzy (hurt an ally on attack), Explode (on faint, 1 dmg/stack split over enemies), Fast/Slow (±1 required speed per stack, consumed on attack), Fatigue, Fortitude (block next hit), Frail, Hidden (untargetable, consumed on attack), Last Wish, Regen, Scrap, Shield, Solid Armour (% reduction), Splash, Strength, Taunt, Thorns (+ Dont_Remove_Thorns variant), Super/Passive/Speed/Health/Damage markers.

## Effect VM
One `ATTACK_EFFECT` enum drives everything: DAMAGE, HEAL, TICK (apply status), SUMMON, SHIELD, EXP, GAIN_GOLD, RECRUIT, SUMMON_ENEMY, SELL_SELF, RANDOM_ENEMY_EFFECT, PLAYER_HEALTH (morale), EAT_AND_STORE, TRIGGER_STORED_EFFECT, ENERGY, TRIGGER_ATTACK, MOVE_SELF, PUSH_BACK, ALL_SUMMONS_DEAL_DAMAGE, TRIGGER_DECAY, TRIGGER_SUPER, GAIN_START_EFFECT, REMOVE_SHIELD_PERCENT, BONUS_HEALTH, COST. Passive rows additionally scale by `percentStat` (`UNIT_STATS`: HP, SPEED, ATTACK, ENERGY, SHIELD, UNIT_OWNED, LOCKED_ITEMS, GOLD, PER_UNIT_BEHIND, PER_ALLY_INFRONT, PER_STATUS, TOTAL_ENEMY_STATUS, AMOUNT_OF_TYPE) and pick their magnitude from `X/Y/Z[stage]`. Passive trigger families (asset counts): OnEnergy (super) 55, OnSpawn 44, OnAttack 16, OnDeath 12, OnHit 12, OnUnitDeath 12, OnAlly{Attack,Hit,Spawn,Heal,Regen,Shield,Super}, OnStatusEffect, OnSelfMove/OnOtherUnitMove, OnGainShield, OnHeal, OnConsume, OnBattleEnd, plus 10 field-effect singletons.

## Progression within a run
- **EXP → evolution**: a unit evolves to stage 1 at **60 EXP** and stage 2 at **180 EXP** (`GetLevel`); some lines evolve by Item (9 `EvolveMethodData` rows) or by team types. EXP sources: +5 to all per battle (`battleTeamEXP`), **+30 by merging a duplicate** (`expForMerge`), +10 to all when a unit is released (`sellEXP`), +5 to all when a reward is skipped (`skipEXP`), +30 to one unit at a camp site (`AddBonusEXPOption`), BONUS EXP passives for same-type units.
- Enemy scaling mirrors the player: every battle fought (won or survived) adds 75 EXP (`enemyEXP`) to `totalExpGained`; the next enemy team's EXP budget equals that total, split per enemy as `budget / count × U(0.6, 1.2)` — so enemies evolve at the same pace a perfectly-merging player would (STRONG INFERENCE on intent, VERIFIED mechanics). Enemy count by encounter index: 1, 2, 3, 4, 4…; from encounter 2 each enemy has an `(encounter−1) × 8 %` chance to hold a random item.
- Rarity weights by encounter index (`unitRarityChances`): encounters 0–2: Common 90 / Rare 10; encounter ≥ 3: Common 65 / Rare 30 / Epic 5. Enemy sampling (`GetRandoUnit`) applies the weight **per eligible unit**, restricted to the current area's pool and excluding tokens, Kadodex-locked units and already-spawned IDs — so with ~20 Common / 8 Rare / 1 Epic lines the effective shares are ≈ 96/4/0 % early and ≈ 84/16/0.3 % late (the UI tooltip shows the raw weights). The shop rolls differently (`FullRandomUnit`: pick a rarity bucket by the enc-≥3 table, then a uniform unit of that rarity over all units).
- **Shop** (`shopUnitCosts`): Common 5, Rare 7, Epic 10 gold; restock costs 1 **ticket** (earned from mini-bosses/bosses; `currentTickets` starts at 1). Items combine via 3 recipes (`ItemRecipeScriptable`). Binning an item returns 1 gold (`UnitBagSlot`); binning (releasing) a unit returns 0 gold, gives +10 EXP to every other unit and returns its held item.
- **Camp site** (rest): per-unit options — +30 EXP, gain a type (Water/Grass/Ice/Physical), gain a starting status (5 STRENGTH "Honey"), gain a passive, unlock a misprint.
- **Misprints**: shiny-style variants (`isMisprint`, `IsAFoundMisprint`) that appear in enemy teams and unlock permanently when saved at a camp.
- **Field effects** (23): run-wide modifiers chosen every 3 battles (Dropped Armour, Bush Fire, Lucky Clover = chance not to consume items, Duality, Frozen, Grounded, Overgrowth = heal on attack, Cursed Chest = 2 random enemies, Rainfall, Weird Tome = gold on damage, Cloudy, Rock Solid = front ally takes less…), implemented as `GlobalEffectHelper` statics.

## Meta progression
Kadodex unlocks (`StaticMonUnlocker.CatchUnit/EvolveUnit`, `UnitData.unlockState`), misprint skins, battle stats per unit (`EORStats`), Steam achievements. Only the first region is available ("prologue" per the REGION tooltip); 12 areas exist as data (Air, Jungle, Boss_Jungle, …) and 3 gym leaders (Water/Grass/Water-typed teams).
