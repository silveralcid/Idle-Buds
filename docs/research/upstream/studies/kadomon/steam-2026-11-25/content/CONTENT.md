# Kadomon — Content Architecture
- 140 `KadomonData` stages: 88 dex-visible (60 Common, 24 Rare, 3 Epic, 1 Token) = 29 three-stage lines (20 Common, 8 Rare, 1 Epic: Frosmol/Freezard/Wyvernity); 52 hidden = 26 EVENT stages (11 names: bosses such as Queenstruction/Acroney/Marvelisk and the gnome family `Gnock/Cannock/Farmock/Sapock/Buffock/Gnockard/Gnocking`, Assisting, Fwoombus), 17 tokens (summons; 9 marked `done:false`), and 9 hidden Common/Rare rows (the level-3 "Hyper" forms).
- 11 types; distribution among visible stages: Water 24, Grass 21, Physical/Fire/Toxic 18, Bug/Dusk/Lightning 15, Air/Earth/Ice 12 — every unit is dual-typed (a few triple), so the 1.25× chart stacks.
- Class pairs: Damage/Damage 21, Support/Support 16, Tank/Support 15, Damage/Mover 6, Support/Summoner 6, Tank/Tank 6…
- 12 keywords (Shelter 6 units, Draining/Battery/Pierce/Stealth/Sniper/Shield Bash 3 each, Fragile 1); 29 traits are flavour tags (Defiant, Happy, Friendly, Coward, Calm…) also usable by `bonusAttackTrait` bonuses.
- 177 `AttackScriptable`, 218 passive assets, 64 status/tooltip definitions, 29 items + 10 consumables + 3 recipes, 23 field effects, 8 rest options, 4 mystery events, 21 map node blueprints (areas with `possibleUnits`, boss unit + boss item, field-effect pools), 3 gym leaders.
- Authoring pipeline: stats tuned in a Google Sheet (`GoogleSheetsForUnity`, tab `FullUnits`) and pushed into ScriptableObjects; descriptions generated from `passiveDesc` templates + `X/Y/Z` arrays; Odin-serialized polymorphic effect rows. Full tables: `evidence/data/kadomon.json|attacks.json|passives.json|*.json`.
