# Palworld — Gameplay Systems
Tiers: VERIFIED = read from exported game data/config; STRONG INFERENCE = community-datamined formula consistent with data fields; HYPOTHESIS = modelled.

## Core loop
Open-world survival + creature collection: explore → weaken and capture Pals with spheres → build a base where Pals work (12 work suitabilities in use) → craft/tech up (588 technologies over levels 1–80) → fight field/tower/raid bosses → breed for species, passives and IVs → level 100 (`DT_PalExpTable` has 100 rows). Four difficulty presets and 46 world-setting fields expose the economy to the player/server (`DT_OptionWorldPresetTable`, `DefaultPalWorldSettings.ini`).

## Pals (VERIFIED data, `evidence/data/pals.{json,csv}`)
288 Paldeck entries (204 base species + 84 subspecies, dex numbers to 204 with a "B" suffix; 12 more rows reuse a dex number for quest/summon/oil-rig variants) among 753 character rows (bosses `BOSS_*`, raid `RAID_*`, gym, predator, summon and quest variants: BOSS 322, PREDATOR 48, GYM 21, RAID 19, SUMMON 4, Quest 2, plain 337). Each species row carries: 1–2 elements (9 types: Normal, Fire, Water, Leaf, Electricity, Ice, Earth, Dark, Dragon), species scaling `Hp/MeleeAttack/ShotAttack/Defense/Support/CraftSpeed` (dex ranges HP 60–200, ATK 50–200, DEF 50–200), size, rarity 1–20, `CaptureRateCorrect` (0.8–1.5), `ExpRatio`, `Price`, `MaleProbability` (10–90), `CombiRank` (30–3 080 in the breeding pool; 10 for the World Tree Dragon, 9 999 for unbreedable rows) + `CombiDuplicatePriority`, `IgnoreCombi`, speeds (walk/run/ride/transport/swim), stamina, stomach/food amount, nocturnal/predator/edible flags, work suitability ranks 1–8 across 12 jobs in use (13 defined), up to 2 fixed passives (4 slots), `Friendship_*` trust scaling, and for enemy variants `EnemyMaxHPRate/ReceiveDamageRate/InflictDamageRate/WazaCoolTimeRate` (e.g. raid Bahamut Dragon: HP ×1.4, takes ×0.4, deals ×2.0). Learn sets: `DT_WazaMasterLevel` (levels at which 342 of the 385 active skills are learned; the rest are boss/unique moves) and `DT_WazaMasterTamago` (inheritable pool). Drops: `DT_PalDropItem` per character/level.

## Combat
- Active skills (`DT_WazaDataTable`, 376 enabled): element, category (Shot/Melee…), `Power` 0–1250, cooldown 0–300 s, ranges, up to two additional effects (status/`Wetness` etc.), `IgnoreRandomInherit`, strength class. Partner skills (50 parameter rows + per-species text).
- Damage (STRONG INFERENCE, community-datamined and consistent with `PalDamageInfo` fields: `BasePower`, `AttackerLevel`, `AttackElementType`, `SneakAttackRate`, `SpecialAttackRateInfos`, PvP rates): `damage ≈ Power × Attack/Defense-style scaling × element multiplier × crit/sneak × world damage rates`; element chart has 2× strengths (Fire>Leaf, Fire>Ice, Water>Fire, Leaf>Earth, Electricity>Water, Ice>Dragon, Earth>Electricity, Dark>Normal, Dragon>Dark) and 0.5× for the reverse — the chart itself is in C++ (not in the exported tables).
- Stats (STRONG INFERENCE, community-derived and widely reproduced; not checkable from the pak): `HP = 500 + 5L + HPbase·0.5·L·(1+IV)`, `ATK = 100 + ATKbase·0.075·L·(1+IV)`, `DEF = 50 + DEFbase·0.075·L·(1+IV)`, each × (1+passives) × (1+souls) × (1+condenser); IV (talent) 0–100 → 0–30 %; `DT_TalentUpItem` fruits add +10 talent; `DT_CharacterUpgradeMasterDataTable` = 20 condenser ranks (1–4 stones per rank across four stone tiers, reset cost 100/300/500… 30 000 gold); `DT_PlayerStatusRankMasterDataTable` = relic ranks (CapturePower 15 ranks needing 1–9 Lifmunk Effigies each). The 500/100/50 bases, 0.5/0.075 scalars and +5 HP/level are not in the tables (C++ constants).
- Capture (VERIFIED inputs, HYPOTHESIS formula): inputs that exist in data — species `CaptureRateCorrect`, world `PalCaptureRate` (0.8–2.0 by preset), 10 sphere tiers (`SPWeaponCaptureBall` items, ranks 1–10, prices 80 → 122 760 gold, plus a debug and a Radar sphere), capture-power relic rank (15 ranks, 100 effigies; the table's `EffectRate` is 0 for every rank, so the per-rank bonus lives in code), sphere modules (`CaptureItemModifier` items), remaining HP, level, status, back-hit bonus; the combination is unpublished and community calculators model it (two "wiggle" rolls, HP-missing term, alpha penalty ≈0.7).
- Enemy scaling: wild spawners (`DT_PalWildSpawner`) give per-group level bands (Common 1–80, FieldBoss 1–79, RandomDungeonBoss 6–69, ImprisonmentBoss 10–66); 263 of 288 dex entries have a wild spawn under their own id (273 counting boss/predator variants); 15 never spawn wild, the rest come from breeding, eggs, dungeons or unique sources.

## Progression
- EXP: `DT_PalExpTable` (VERIFIED): player total 5 510 at L10, 2.38 M at L50, 10.6 M at L65, 382 M at L100; Pal totals exceed the player's from L49 to L97 (3.9× at L65, 3.2× at L80) because `PalNextEXP` is flat at 6.9 M from L65 up — at L100 the Pal total (283 M) is again below the player's (382 M). Per-row `DropEXP`, `BuildEXP`, `CraftEXP` and Pal variants; capture bonus EXP by count (`DT_PalCaptureBonusExpTable`, 4 999 rows: catching the 1st…n-th of a species).
- Technology (VERIFIED): 588 nodes, 537 regular (1 413 points total) + 51 boss technologies (182 ancient points), tiers by level up to 80, with `RequireDefeatTowerBoss`/`RequireResearchId` gates (Lab research table).
- Base: `DT_BaseCampLevelData` 35 levels → worker cap 1…, bases per guild 1→2 at level 8…; `DT_BaseCampTask`, worker events/sickness tables.
- Trust/Friendship (VERIFIED): rank thresholds 6 000 / 13 000 / 21 000 / 30 000 / 40 000 / 55 000 / 80 000 / 110 000 / 150 000 / 200 000 points (ranks −3…10), feeding `Friendship_HP/ShotAttack/Defense` scalars per species.
- Breeding (VERIFIED fields, STRONG INFERENCE rule): child = pool Pal whose `CombiRank` is nearest `floor((rankA+rankB+1)/2)`, ties → higher rank, shared ranks → `CombiDuplicatePriority`; 258 unique parent combinations override; 260 species in the generic pool, all but Aegidron reachable from two different parents (`simulations/palworld_model.py`: 18 044 rank-decided, 15 551 tie-decided, 152 unique, 183 same-species pairs); `MaleProbability` sets egg gender; `DA_BreedingItemEffectData` = cake tiers (mutation chance).
