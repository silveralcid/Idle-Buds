# Palworld — Economy
Gold (`Money` item, stack 99 999 999) from selling Pals (`Price` 313–19 900 per species row) and items (2 466 items with `Price`: Wood/Stone 12, Pal Sphere 80, Ingot 240, Cake 6 300, Legendary Sphere 5 570, Ancient Sphere `PalSphere_Ancient_2` 122 760), merchant tables (`DT_ItemShop*`, `DT_PalShopCreateData`), arena rewards. Sinks: shop purchases, condenser resets (100/300/500… → 30 000 gold over 20 ranks; relic-statue reset is a nominal 1 gold), technology is point-based (1 413 + 182 ancient), crafting is work-based (Pal work speed × suitability; cake 200 000 work units). Loot: 8 782 item-lottery rows (chests/dungeons), per-Pal drop tables (1 029 rows), world rates `CollectionDropRate`/`EnemyDropItemRate` (0.5–2.0 by preset). Premium: none in-client (paid title, DLC cosmetics/skins via `DT_SkinDataTable`); the whole economy is tunable server-side (`DefaultPalWorldSettings.ini`).
