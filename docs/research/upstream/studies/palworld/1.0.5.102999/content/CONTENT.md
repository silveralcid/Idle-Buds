# Palworld — Content Architecture
Everything is UE DataTables + DataAssets + Blueprints (data-driven, no scripting language): 503 tables / 197 731 rows exported. Headline counts (VERIFIED): 753 character rows → 288 Paldeck entries (204 species + 84 subspecies; 12 further rows reuse a dex number for quest/summon/oil-rig variants; element mix Dark 76, Leaf 47, Water 47, Earth 46, Fire 44, Ice 38, Normal 38, Dragon 26, Electricity 25; rarity 1–10 plus 20 for legendaries), 385 active skills (376 enabled; Dark 56, Normal 54, Leaf 40…; power 0–1 250, cooldown 0–300 s), 417 localized passives (313 distinct names; a further 56 rows carry the placeholder "en Text") (effect types: ShotAttack 48, Defense 30, element boosts ≈190, CraftSpeed 20, MoveSpeed 11, drop boosts…), 50 partner-skill parameter rows, 2 466 items (Blueprint 603, Weapon 388, Armor 355, Essential 334, Consume 237, Material 214, Accessory 145, Food 124, Ammo 38, SpecialWeapon 12, Glider 8, CaptureItemModifier 7), 1 414 recipes, 588 technologies, 1 034 map objects, 1 691 wild spawner rows (413 spawner groups with Pals, 8 253 placements), 258 unique breeding combos, 27 English text tables (≈15 k strings). Authoring pattern: `_Common` tables hold data, `DT_X` composites layer overrides; names/descriptions are keyed text rows (`PAL_NAME_<id>`, `ITEM_NAME_<id>`, `PASSIVE_<id>`); enemy variants reuse species rows with rate multipliers; subspecies share dex numbers with suffixes.
