# Palworld — Progression
Player level 1–100 (`DT_PalExpTable`, VERIFIED) gates technology (588 nodes; points per level; boss techs on ancient points from tower bosses), stat points (HP/SP/attack/work speed/weight via level and elixir items `DT_GainStatusPointsItem`), and base level (35 base levels raise worker cap and bases per guild). Pals progress by level (XP, capture bonus), **trust** (10 ranks, 6 000→200 000 points, scaling HP/attack/defense per species), **condensing** (20 ranks, `DT_CharacterUpgradeMasterDataTable`), **souls/statue** (relic ranks), **talents/IVs** (+10 per fruit), **passives** (up to 4 slots, species fix at most 2; 417 localized passives, ranks −3…5 with lottery weights), **skills** (learn by level, egg inheritance, skill fruits) and **breeding** (species funnel + 258 unique combos; mutated eggs via cake tiers). Collection: Paldeck 288 entries, 263 of which spawn wild under their own id; the rest come from breeding/eggs/dungeons/unique sources. Difficulty presets and 46 world settings (`world_presets.json`) rescale every curve.
