# Idle Clans — Game Design & Player Psychology (STRONG INFERENCE)

## Why it works
1. **Borrowed grammar, lower cost.** Skills, XP table that doubles every 7 levels, mastery capes at 99/120, slayer (Exterminating), bosses with weighted drops, a player market — the OSRS mental model, with the time-cost replaced by timers and offline progress. Players arrive already knowing what "99 Woodcutting" means.
2. **Flat rates, exponential goals.** Because XP/h barely rises with level while requirements double every 7 levels, every boost (tools, capes, housing, clan house, potions, premium) is worth the same *percentage* forever — purchases never go obsolete and the optimisation puzzle stays alive to L120.
3. **Multiplayer where idle games are usually lonely.** Clans convert individual grind into shared sinks (clan house 500 M), shared buffs (clan quests), shared fights (bosses, raids, turkey events) and status (Clan Cup, leaderboards, titles) — relatedness and competence loops that single-player idlers lack.
4. **Two idle lanes.** Your character runs one task; pets run another; quests and daily ad boosts add appointment beats; the 12 h offline cap sets a two-logins-a-day rhythm without punishing a missed day.
5. **Honest economy.** Server authority + a 1 % tax market + bonds-style tradeable premium make "F2P can earn premium" literally true (1 M gold), which the reviews cite as the key trust factor.

## Where it strains
1. **The 120 wall.** L99→120 is 7× the XP of 1→99 with no new verbs; the only new content per skill is one 10–60× slower task. Engagement past 99 relies on completionism and leaderboards (obligation-flavoured).
2. **Premium gating of breadth** (three skills capped at 30, three task groups) is the monetization pressure point that generates most negative sentiment, even though the gate is gold-passable.
3. **Flat gathering gold/h** means money comes from bossing and market play; pure skillers fund progress slowly, pushing everyone toward the same combat loop.
4. **Information load**: 1 000 items, 49 upgrades with 1–190 tiers, 21 clan upgrades, potions with chance/strength/duration triples — the wiki is effectively required; the client's popup-heavy UI is a symptom.
5. **Appointment mechanics** (ads ×4/day, 12 h cap, daily quests, weekly clan quests, monthly cup) are retention-first; they are mild individually but stack into a schedule.

## Enjoyment vs obligation
Genuine: planning boost stacks, market trading, group bosses, clan cooperation. Obligation-driven: offline-cap logins, ad boosts, capping all skills at 120, Clan Cup pressure on members. The game sits mid-genre ethically: no gacha, no energy, transparent odds (weights are public), but it does sell breadth and time.
