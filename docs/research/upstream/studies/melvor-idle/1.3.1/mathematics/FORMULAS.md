## Model results (`simulations/melvor_model.py`)
- **XP curve**: identical to Koltera 2's table up to 99 (both use the RuneScape formula); the base game's level cap is 99 and expansions lift it to 120, which costs 8× the XP of 1→99.
- **Base skilling rates** (base interval, no mastery, gear or modifiers): Woodcutting tops out at 43 200 XP/h (Redwood); Fishing at 106 000 (Whale, after its 10 % junk); sustained Mining is limited by rock HP and respawn (Dragonite ≈ 17 000–19 000 XP/h at 5 HP, ≈ 97 000 with +99 HP from mastery, and it needs 270 total Mining mastery first); Thieving King succeeds only 18.6–19 % at stealth 95–99, ≈ 16 300–16 700 XP/h with Continue-on-Stun on. Artisan skills are input-bound and spread widely: Smithing up to 900 000 XP/h, Runecrafting 810 000, Fletching 630 000, Crafting 480 000, Herblore 324 000, Cooking 317 000, Firemaking 105 000, Summoning 29 500. One-tree Woodcutting to 99 takes 404 h; Fishing 175 h with junk; sustained Mining ≈ 320–340 h.
- **Mastery** scales with the skill's action count: the `masteryLevel·actions/10` term means a skill with 115 actions (Smithing) earns ~13× the mastery XP per action of a 9-action skill, so one Smithing action reaches mastery 99 in ~8.5 h (1.7 s mastery interval, 2 s actions) versus ~92 h for Woodcutting. For skills that use the real action interval, total mastery per skill comes out roughly equal (~790 h sequential for Woodcutting, Fishing or a 3 s Smithing, per the review's simulation); the fixed artisan mastery intervals break that (Smithing/Runecrafting ≈ 930 h, Fletching ≈ 1 210 h).
- **Combat**: hit chance is continuous and symmetric around A = E (50 %): 2× accuracy gives 75 %, 4× gives 87.5 %, half gives 25 %. A level-99 player with +150 strength bonus has a 392 melee max hit (39.2 in "×10" units).
- **Thieving** success is level-insensitive at the top: Court Jester (perception 715) goes from 22.0 % at level 79 to 24.4 % at 99, so stealth from sources other than level (chiefly per-NPC mastery, +75 at mastery 99) carries late Thieving.
- **Offline replays online**: for every action that keeps running offline, offline runs the same tick function (up to 24 h, 1 728 000 ticks), so it has the online behaviour; by default combat is stopped when going offline, so combat progress offline needs the opt-in setting.
