## Implications (from `simulations/siralim_model.py`)
- Stats are **linear in level** with slope `0.3·base` (0.2–0.4 by personality): a base-50 health creature has 1 535 HP at L100 and 15 035 at L1000; the personality swing is ±33 % of the level-dependent part, so personality matters more than ±1 base point past level ~10.
- Base stats are tightly banded (sum 99–150, mean 110; 394 creatures sum to exactly 110, 279 to 105), so species identity is the **trait**, not the numbers — consistent with the trait text being 5.7 MB of localization.
- XP is quadratic (`20L²`): level 100 = 200 000 XP, 1 000 = 20 M; the level cap is 50 + 10·gene strength (lifted for nether creatures), so gene strength is the cap currency; with realm depth driving enemy levels quadratically, the design keeps `enemy level ≈ realm depth` until realm 33 (clamp), then the `0.03·R²` term takes over: realm 100 → level 330, realm 500 → 11 250, realm 1 000 → 60 000 (×3 mode: 180 000). Gene strength (+10 % stat per point in `plus`) follows at (level−50)/10.
- Crit and dodge are pure **speed-ratio** games before modifiers: both start at 5 %; double speed = 10 % crit; the defender needs 6× the attacker's speed to hit the 30 % dodge cap, and a slower defender still keeps the 5 % floor.
