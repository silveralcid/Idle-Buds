# Gameplay systems
## Core loop (VERIFIED by content/scripts; descriptions STRONG INFERENCE)
Castle hub → choose a realm (god-themed biome, `scr_GetRaceArrayByRealm`, 20+ Nether realms) at a chosen **realm depth** → explore a generated floor (`scr_create_rooms/corridors`, fog of war, realm quests `scr_GetRandomStartingRealmQuest`, realm properties/buffs `scr_GetRandomRealmBuff/Debuff`) → battles vs packs scaled by depth (`scr_GetEnemyLevelByRealmLevel`, `scr_CreateRandomEnemyCreature`, `scr_GenerateSpawnList`) → loot (`inv_Loot`, `inv_LootBox`, `inv_LootRoll`, materials, spell gems, artifacts, cards) → back to the castle to fuse (`scr_Fuse`), breed (`scr_GenerateBreedingList`), enchant (`inv_ArtifactEnchant`), spend god favour (`scr_GodShopSetup`, 1.8 MB) and specialization perks (`scr_PerkLevel`).
## Creatures (VERIFIED data)
1 447 rows: 5 classes (Sorcery 335, Nature 319, Chaos 278, Death 275, Life 240), 166 races (Exotic 148, Avatar 104, Godspawn 31, …). Five stats with tight bands: health 21–50, attack 2–30, intelligence 5–28, defense 6–30, speed 7–30; total 99–150 (394 creatures at exactly 110). Each row carries exactly one innate trait id (2 181 trait rows, 2 157 localized, 23 keys missing from the CSV). Fusion averages base stats (`round((a+b)/2)`) and stacks both traits (`fuse2`); which parent's sprite/race is kept was not read from `scr_Fuse` (community knowledge: the first). Gene strength (`plus`) sets the level cap (50 + 10·plus); where it scales stats was not found in `scr_GetCreatureStat` (UNKNOWN). Personality picks the favoured/unfavoured stat (0.4/0.2 vs 0.3).
## Battle (structure STRONG INFERENCE from script names and decompiled chance functions)
6 v 6 (community knowledge), turn order by `turnspeed`; actions attack / cast / defend / provoke / extract (`bc_ExtractChance`). Damage pipeline `bc_EventDamage` (3.1 MB) with crit (`bc_GetCriticalChance`), dodge (`bc_GetDodgeChance`), min/max stat clamps (`bc_CreatureMinimumStat/MaximumStat`), status ticks (bleed/burn/poison/bomb/mania calculators), buff/debuff pools (`bc_GetRandomBuff/Debuff`), minions, spell gems with properties (`inv_SpellGemSetProperties`, 30 property flags per spell row), class advantage handled in `bc_SpellDamage`/`bc_CreatureAttack` (not isolated here). Every trait is bespoke code in the `bc_*` hooks (`bc_StartupTraits` 134 KB, `bc_CreatureStartOfTurn` 1.7 MB).
## Spells (VERIFIED data)
747 spell-gem rows: Chaos 150, Sorcery 145, Death 137, Nature 128, Life 117, plus 70 rows (ids 678–747, `SpellGems5`) whose layout differs and is only partly decoded (tags recovered, class not); type tags DAMAGE 273, UTILITY 174, DEBUFF 103, BOOST 80, BUFF 69, HEAL 42, DEBOOST 39, ULTIMATE 31, MINION 25, ARTIFACT 25; field 2 = mana/charges (10 most common, 15, 5, 12, 7, 20, 1, 8…).
## Specializations & perks (VERIFIED data)
661 perk rows (the Feb-2023 community dataset lists 41 specializations; not re-derived here); max rank 1 for 440 perks, 10/25/5/20/3 for the rest; cost field 100 for 369 (percent-style perks), 10/1/4/5/20/50 otherwise. Anointments (`scr_AnointmentsListByGod/BySpec`) let a specialization borrow others' perks.
## Items (VERIFIED data)
1 862 materials (1 800 of type 2 = trait materials, 47 type 1, 15 type 0), 388 accessories, 834 skins (with base-creature link), 43 runes, 22 cooking recipes, 354 quests; items.csv 3 220 strings; artifacts are generated (`inv_CreateArtifact`, `inv_ArtifactRollRange`, `inv_ArtifactStatRarity`, 5-ish slots `inv_ArtifactPrimarySlots`) rather than tabled.
## Modes (2.0 notes + script names)
Skip Story, Ironman, Sandbox (`scr_SandboxLoad`), Royal, Calm, Randomizer (`global.mode` 1/2 scale enemy levels ×0.65/×3), arena/brawl (`scr_ArenaShopSetup`, `scr_BrawlCreateCreatures`), menagerie duels.
