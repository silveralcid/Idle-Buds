# Economy
Currencies (VERIFIED names in text/scripts): Brimstone, Crystal, Essence, Granite, Power (resources from realms, `L_BRIMSTONE…`), Piety/god favour, Stardust & Glamour (2.0), emblems/cards, arena/brawl tokens. Sinks: fusion & breeding, artifact enchanting/salvage (`inv_ArtifactEnchant/Salvage/Disenchant`), spell-gem transmutation (`inv_SpellGemTransmute`), god shops (`scr_GodShopSetup`, 1.8 MB of stock), guild shops (Life/Nature/Chaos/Death/Sorcery `scr_<Class>GuildShopSetup`, 41 KB each), tavern merchant (`scr_RefreshTavernMerchant`), decorations (2 775 strings, 4 database scripts of 2.2–3.0 MB), costumes/skins (834), projects (258 strings) that unlock castle NPCs, Goblet of Giving re-rolls (5 000 essence doubling, per the published patch notes, not verified here). Loot: `inv_Loot`/`inv_LootBox` with per-realm property boosts, daily loot (`inv_DailyLoot`), material rarity (`inv_MaterialRarity`), nether stones (`inv_NetherStoneRarity/GetStat`). No real-money purchases; paid Steam title (bundles with the earlier Siralim games). Costs/drop rates themselves live inside the 36 KB `inv_Loot` and shop setup scripts and were not itemised (UNKNOWN: exact drop tables).
