# Development lessons
Adopt: (1) **data as code is fine for a solo dev** — `scr_Database*` scripts give compile-time checking and one language, and still proved decodable; (2) **macro tokens in text** (`{STAT_health}`) decouple localization from design changes; (3) **linear stats × quadratic enemy curve** makes depth the difficulty dial while keeping every level meaningful; (4) a **speed-ratio crit/dodge** rule is one line yet creates a real stat trade-off; (5) **base-stat budget ≈ 110** keeps 1 447 creatures balanced by trait rather than by numbers.
Avoid: (1) hand-writing every trait into shared hooks (`bc_CreatureStartOfTurn` 1.7 MB, `bc_EventDamage` 3.1 MB) — a trait DSL (see Super Auto Pets) would cut bug surface and make balance tooling possible; (2) BigNumbers for every stat op (two extension calls per formula) when doubles suffice below 1e15; (3) shipping dev tooling (GMLive server, debug flags) in release; (4) 226 placeholder trait rows and the `SpellGems5` layout drift show the database-as-code approach needs a validator.
