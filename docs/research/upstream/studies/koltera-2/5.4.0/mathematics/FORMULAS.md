# Formulas

Every formula below is read from the original TypeScript recovered from the shipped source map (`evidence/source_reconstructed/`, paths relative to it; all 207 files match the map's `sourcesContent` byte for byte). Rows 1–16, 20–28 and 30 are exercised numerically by `simulations/k2_model.py` (output in `simulations/k2_model_output.txt`); rows 17–19, 29, 31 and 32 are documented rules the model prints but does not simulate. Tiers: **VERIFIED** = in the shipped source; **VERIFIED (data)** = a constant in the content/config tables dumped to `evidence/data/`; **computed** = a consequence calculated by the model (values quoted inside VERIFIED rows are computed from the verified rule).

## Model results (`simulations/k2_model.py`)
- **Gathering to 99** with the best unlocked activity at every level: 1 125 h at the in-game minimum (player level 1, +0.5 %), about 1 098 h for a first skill (player level ≤ 11), 1 005 h at an average +12.5 % player bonus, 204 h with every other bonus maxed at player level 11 and 191 h with player level 99 (XP ×3.55, cycles ×0.6). Level 50 takes 33 h base / 5.6–6.0 h maxed; 90 takes 690 h / 117–125 h. The last rung (L90, 220 XP per 45 s) nearly doubles XP/h over the L80 rung (17 600 vs 9 257 base).
- **Universal drops** come out at a constant 90 / 45 / 4.5 per hour on every rung, because the chance scales with base duration; the exception is the common drop on the L90 rung (chance 1.125, so certain: 80/h base). Duration reductions do not lower the chance, so the maxed rate is 150 / 75 / 7.5 drops per hour, and +3 yield makes each drop 4 items: **30 containers (charms) per hour per skill** at maxima versus 4.5 at base, a 6.7× swing produced by two "generic" bonuses.
- **Sanctuary**: only awakened creatures qualify, so these lineups need eight awakened (level-70-passed) tier-4/5 creatures. Every job can reach tier 5 with its best eight (73–75 points against 54), and up to three jobs can be tier 5 at once, but not all six: the eight highest job totals sum to 271 points, a tier sum of 26 needs 276, so the best all-round lineup is 5/4/4/4/4/4 (sum 25 of 30, found by annealing; the review confirmed the three-job maximum and the 25 ceiling with an exact integer program, and found 23 as the best tier sum when three jobs are tier 5). Lineup choice is a real allocation decision: specialise three jobs to tier 5 or spread to tier 4 everywhere.
- **Creatures**: before awakening, XP comes from machines (0.5 XP/s: 136 h to L70), helpers (0.40–0.95 XP/s base, 3.61 maxed: 72 h / 18.9 h), expeditions and dungeons. Expedition type 1 has a base rating of 1, so any party gets the 5-minute minimum: solo it pays 1.0 XP/s at tier 1 and 1.8 XP/s at tier 5 (reached after 50 runs, ~4.2 h), up to 3.24 XP/s with the loop bonus and a level-10 sword, i.e. L70 in ~38 h for one creature; expedition XP is split by party size and the UI runs one expedition per type. Dungeons pay a flat 450·tier XP per creature per 15 min (0.5–2.5 XP/s each) but cost an armor set per creature. The 120-node upgrade tree costs exactly 120 awaken points, one per species (there are exactly 120 species), i.e. 29.4 M creature XP.
- **Summoning inputs**: the main-output gathered resources for all 120 species are 13 101 h of player-only base gathering (1 965 h at single-skill maxima); tier 5 is 57 % of that. Because helpers are uncapped, every summoned creature adds throughput: all 120 on their best job equal 55.7 players (an upper bound: sanctuary members cannot help, and in parallel work most jobs get +2 rather than +3 yield). A labour-only snowball estimate (player + all earlier creatures as helpers) gives about 583 h base / 87 h maxed, but it ignores level gates: helpers can only work activities the player has unlocked, tier-3 inputs come from the L60/L70 rungs, tier 4 from L80 and tier 5 from L90, and helpers give no player XP. Reaching L90 in all six gathering skills takes about 4 040 h base / 700 h maxed, so player skill levels, not input volume, pace the roster. The 30 596 charms are 1 020 maxed player-only hours, or about 41 M gold of charm crates (the scarcest type, Exploring, binds). Tier-4/5 summons also need 48 000 dungeon runes: at least 2 000 S-grade tier-5 dungeon runs and as many armor sets.
- **Expeditions** never fail: the party score only moves duration between 60 and 5 minutes, and loop bonuses cap at +20 % after 200 loops. **Dungeons** grade by a ratio, S at 2× the tier rating.
- **Machines** at L10 run at 0.28× interval: generators 225 items/h, processors 109 conversions/h, for 100 k–800 k gold each.
- **Garden**: clearing all rocks costs 307 532 gold; a full garden of L6 gold flowers makes 150 gold/min, so the rocks repay in 34 h and the whole build with merchant flowers and fertilizer (838 782 gold) in 93 h.
- **Loop drift**: completing an activity resets `startTime = now`, dropping the overshoot (on average half the 33 ms dispatch interval): 0.34 % slower than nominal at the shortest reachable cycle (8 s × 0.6 = 4.8 s), 0.2 % at 8 s.
- **Offline differs from online in both directions**: gathering XP is floored per cycle offline (xpRate 2 with +12.5 %: 2 instead of 2.25, −11 %; bonuses under +50 % on the first rung are lost), but offline processors consume only their primary input and offline machine creatures gain XP even when the machine is idle; see ARCHITECTURE for the full list.
