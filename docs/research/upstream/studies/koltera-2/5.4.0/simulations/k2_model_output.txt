## 2. Player level and its XP bonus
player level = floor(sum of 9 skill levels / 9), cap 99; bonus % = level*0.25 + 0.25
  player level  1: +0.50% XP
  player level 10: +2.75% XP
  player level 50: +12.75% XP
  player level 99: +25.00% XP

## 3. Bonus stacks at their maxima (modules/bonuses/helpers.ts, content/configs/sanctuary.ts, tools, upgrades)
sanctuary T5: XP +120%, duration -20%, yield +1  (source comments say +20%/+40% XP; the numbers are 40 and 80 -> +120% total)
upgrade tree per gathering skill: XP +60.0%, duration -20.0%, yield +2.0
tool L10: +50% XP; player L99: +25%
=> max gathering XP multiplier 3.55x, duration multiplier 0.60, +3 items on every successful drop

## 16. Loop timing and offline (views/GameWrapper.tsx, utils/offline-progress.ts)
  requestAnimationFrame loop; per-frame delta clamped to 1 s; fixed update every 1 s via accumulator; state dispatch throttled to 33 ms
  activities complete when Date.now()-startTime >= duration, then startTime = now -> residual time is dropped each cycle
    duration  4.8s: mean loss ~16.5 ms/cycle (half the 33 ms dispatch) = 0.34% slower than nominal
    duration    8s: mean loss ~16.5 ms/cycle (half the 33 ms dispatch) = 0.21% slower than nominal
    duration   45s: mean loss ~16.5 ms/cycle (half the 33 ms dispatch) = 0.04% slower than nominal
    the shortest reachable cycle is 8 s x 0.6 = 4.8 s (player and job-10 helpers), so the largest drift is ~0.34 %; 72 h offline replays at most 72*3600/4.8 = 54 000 cycles per gatherer
  frame gaps between 1 s and 10 s: each frame is clamped to 1 s, so fixed-update systems and helper XP lose time while wall-clock systems do not
  sleep >= 10 s detected by wall clock -> offline calculator; offline cap 72 h
  offline vs online divergences (utils/offline-progress.ts):
    gathering XP floored per cycle offline (xpRate 2 at +12.5 %: 2 vs 2.25 online); crafting XP floored per job offline, rounded online
    processors consume only their primary input offline (bakery leather/egg, refinery stone are never taken)
    machine creatures gain 0.5 XP/s offline whether or not the machine runs; online only while it operates
    a not-yet-completed gathering/helper cycle subtracts the simulated time from startTime a second time
    the garden discards partial progress when no cycle completes; the sleep path starts from lastSaveTime, up to 30 s stale, re-crediting time already simulated
