## 2. Bucketed tick lists (Verse/TickList.cs, Verse/Thing.cs DoTick)
Normal things tick every tick; Rare things are hashed into 250 buckets and Long things into 2 000 buckets by GetHashCode(),
and each tick runs one bucket. Per-tick work for N things (ILLUSTRATIVE counts):
    1000 rare things:    4.0 TickRare() calls per tick on average (instead of 1000 every 250th tick)
    5000 rare things:   20.0 TickRare() calls per tick on average (instead of 5000 every 250th tick)
   20000 rare things:   80.0 TickRare() calls per tick on average (instead of 20000 every 250th tick)
  5 000 random hashes into 250 buckets: min 9, mean 20, max 33 per tick
Since 1.6, Normal-ticker things also get TickInterval(delta) at a camera-dependent rate (GenTicks.GetCameraUpdateRate):
  on screen in the current map: every (zoom + 1) ticks; off screen or another map: every 15 ticks, clamped to [1, 15].
  closest zoom    : TickInterval every  1 ticks ->  60.0 interval updates per second, 100.0 % of per-tick cost
  mid zoom        : TickInterval every  3 ticks ->  20.0 interval updates per second,  33.3 % of per-tick cost
  far / off screen: TickInterval every 15 ticks ->   4.0 interval updates per second,   6.7 % of per-tick cost
Pawns split their work: every tick = pathing, verbs, stances, job driver, health, equipment, abilities, sound;
TickInterval(delta) = job checks, health interval, mind state, needs (every 150 ticks via IsHashIntervalTick(150, delta)),
apparel, social interactions, skills, relations, psychic entropy … (Verse/Pawn.cs Tick 1538-1617, TickInterval 1618-).

## 3. A* pathfinding as in Verse/PathFinderJob.cs (Burst job), heuristic strength experiment
Costs: cardinal step = pawn TicksPerMoveCardinal (default 13), diagonal = TicksPerMoveDiagonal (default 18), plus cell cost;
cells cost >= 10 000 are impassable, blocked cells +175; diagonals may not cut corners; octile heuristic x 13 x strength;
a closed node is reopened only if the new cost beats it by more than ceil(0.8 x 13) = 11.
Strength: colonists 1 (1.5 in unnatural darkness), animals 1.75, other AI pawns round(curve(40 -> 1, 120 -> 2)) by distance (PathFinder.cs 535-555).
30 random 90x90 maps, 28 % walls (ILLUSTRATIVE): path cost and nodes expanded relative to strength 1
  strength 1.00: cost x1.000 (worst x1.000), nodes expanded x1.00
  strength 1.50: cost x1.043 (worst x1.155), nodes expanded x0.16
  strength 1.75: cost x1.063 (worst x1.155), nodes expanded x0.14
  strength 2.00: cost x1.075 (worst x1.189), nodes expanded x0.13
  note: 13 x 1.41421 = 18.38 > 18, so even strength 1 slightly overestimates diagonal moves (weighted A*, not strictly optimal).
Requests are asynchronous: PathRequest objects are queued, gathered at MapPreTick, solved as Burst jobs (a parallel grid job per
distinct cost-grid request, then one path job per request) and resolved at the start of the next tick; FindPathNow is the synchronous path.

## 4. Regions, reachability and closest-thing search (Verse/Region*.cs, Reachability.cs, GenClosest.cs)
  map 200x200:  40000 cells,  289 12x12 region grid squares (regions never span a square, so >= this many on open ground)
  map 250x250:  62500 cells,  441 12x12 region grid squares (regions never span a square, so >= this many on open ground)
  map 325x325: 105625 cells,  784 12x12 region grid squares (regions never span a square, so >= this many on open ground)
  ClosestThingReachable: breadth-first over regions up to 30 regions (about 4 300 cells if full squares), then a global scan of the
  lister with a reachability check; reachability uses an immediate check, a same-district shortcut, then region flood fill with a
  cache keyed by (district pair, traverse parms). Regions and rooms are rebuilt lazily from dirty cells in MapUpdate.

## 6. Mental breaks (Verse.AI/MentalBreaker.cs)
Thresholds from stat MentalBreakThreshold (base 0.35): minor 35 %, major 35 x 4/7 = 20 %, extreme 35/7 = 5 % mood.
Checked every 150 ticks; mood must stay below a threshold for more than 2 000 ticks, then MTB 4 / 0.8 / 0.5 days.
Rand.MTBEventOccurs uses p = check / (mtb x unit) per check (linear, not 1 - e^-x).
  minor  : P(break) after 6 h below   5.2 %, 24 h  21.4 %, 72 h  52.4 %
  major  : P(break) after 6 h below  23.6 %, 24 h  70.1 %, 72 h  97.6 %
  extreme: P(break) after 6 h below  35.0 %, 24 h  85.6 %, 72 h  99.7 %
  recovery from one break blocks a new random break for 15 000 ticks (6 h).

## 11. Raid lord (Verse.AI.Group/Lord.cs, RimWorld/LordJob_AssaultColony.cs, FactionDef.cs)
  every toil of a non-player auto-flee faction gets a high-priority transition to PanicFlee when the fraction of pawns lost
  reaches a value drawn once per lord, seeded by its loadID, from attackersDownPercentageRangeForAutoFlee (default 0.4-0.7).
  assault gives up after 26 000-38 000 ticks (10.4-15.2 h), sappers/breachers after 33 000-38 000; leaves "satisfied" once
  25-35 % of colony value is damaged; kidnap and steal subgraphs trigger on downed colonists or valuables nearby.
  the lord graph is built under Rand seeded with loadID x 193, so a given lord rebuilds identically.

## 13. Job choice (Data/Core/Defs/ThinkTreeDefs/Humanlike.xml, ThinkNode_PrioritySorter.cs, JobGiver_*.cs)
Main tree is a strict priority list (downed, burning, critical mental states, close melee threat, mental states, queued job,
drafted orders, self-tend, lord duty, prisoner logic, seek allowed area/safe temperature, ...) ending in MainColonistBehaviorCore,
whose ThinkNode_PrioritySorter shuffles its children (random tie-break) and tries them in order of GetPriority():
  giver   | condition                                   | Anything | Work | Joy | Sleep
  GetFood | food < race want-eat level                  |      9.5 |  9.5 | 9.5 | 9.5
  GetRest | rest < 0.30 (Sleep slot: always)            |        8 |    0 |   8 | 8
  GetJoy  | joy < 0.35 (Joy slot: < 0.95; Sleep slot: < 0.95) |        6 |    0 |   7 | 2
  Work    | -                                           |      5.5 |    9 |   2 | 3
  (GetRest in the Joy slot: 8 if rest < 0.3; Meditate slot: rest < 0.16 -> 8, work 2.) The constant think tree (flee explosions,
  find oxygen, hostility response, lord constant duty) is evaluated every 30 ticks and may interrupt the current job.
JobGiver_Work walks work givers in (player priority, work type natural priority, priorityInType) order and stops at the first
priorityInType tier that yields a target; scanners search region-wise for the closest reachable thing, or globally by priority.

## 16. Market value, trade prices and research time (StatWorker_MarketValue.cs, TradeUtility.cs, Tradeable.cs, ResearchManager.cs)
crafted value = sum(ingredients) + work x 0.0036: assault rifle 60 steel + 7 components + 40 000 work = 482; revolver 135
quality factors on market value: awful 0.5, poor 0.75, normal 1, good 1.25 (gain <= 500), excellent 1.5 (<= 1000), masterwork 2.5 (<= 2000), legendary 5 (<= 3000)
player sells at MV x 0.6 x (1 + negotiator); buys at MV x 1.4 x (1 - negotiator); sell is clamped to <= buy; Hard/Extreme also cut sell and raise buy by 10/20 %.
negotiator = TradePriceImprovement = 0.015 x Social level x talking/hearing capacity (max 0.395)
  Social | gain  | buy x MV | sell x MV | round-trip loss
  L 0    | 0.000 |    1.400 |     0.600 |  57.1 %
  L 5    | 0.075 |    1.295 |     0.645 |  50.2 %
  L10    | 0.150 |    1.190 |     0.690 |  42.0 %
  L15    | 0.225 |    1.085 |     0.735 |  32.3 %
  L20    | 0.300 |    0.980 |     0.780 |  20.4 %
  at the 0.395 cap: buy 0.847, sell 0.837 -> the gap never closes, so trade cannot be a loop by itself
research points per tick = ResearchSpeed x bench factor x 0.00825 x difficulty factor / cost factor;
ResearchSpeed = (0.08 + 0.115 x Intellectual) x WorkSpeedGlobal x capacities; benches: simple 0.75, hi-tech 1.0 (x multi-analyzer etc.)
cost factor: 1 + 0.5 per tech level the project is above the colony, with the project tech capped at Industrial
  project              cost | hours at the bench: L6 simple / L10 hi-tech / L15 hi-tech (industrial colony; tribal x factor)
  Electricity          1600 |    134 /     63 /     43 h  (Industrial; tribal start x2.0)
  Gunsmithing           500 |     42 /     20 /     13 h  (Industrial; tribal start x2.0)
  MicroelectronicsBasics   3000 |    252 /    118 /     81 h  (Industrial; tribal start x2.0)
  MultiAnalyzer        4000 |    336 /    158 /    107 h  (Industrial; tribal start x2.0)
  Fabrication          4000 |    336 /    158 /    107 h  (Industrial; tribal start x2.0)
  ShipBasics           4000 |    336 /    158 /    107 h  (Spacer; tribal start x2.0)
  ShipReactor          6000 |    504 /    237 /    161 h  (Spacer; tribal start x2.0)
research also trains Intellectual at 0.1 XP per tick (250 XP per hour before passion).
