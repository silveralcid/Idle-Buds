# Progression
Spine: per-skill XP table to level 200 (STRONG INFERENCE copy: L50 93 k, L80 1.69 M, L100 10 M, L125 100 M, L150 1.38 B, L200 100 B). Content gates end around level 95–106 (gear and recipe requirements), so levels past ~110 are pure efficiency (1 %/level above requirement) and leaderboard standing. Unlock cadence: gathering tiers every 10–15 levels to 80; production recipes to 106; combat zones by monster level (combat level 3 → several hundred; the highest defined level, 3 576, is the Deranged Jester dungeon boss; all 18 labyrinth and guild monsters are defined at level 100 and scaled in proportion to room or trial level, `lr = 100` l.26269, `getProportionallyScaledCombatDetails` l.26363); Labyrinth at total level 1 000; ability slots at Intelligence 20/50/90. Horizontal progression: house rooms, achievements tiers, collection/bestiary points, guild buildings, enhancement levels on gear (+10 is ~1 k attempts with protection, +20 ~4 M — the real endgame sink). Combat tiers T1–T5 rescale the same monsters, so content stays relevant as levels rise.
