# Economy
Currencies: coins (vendor sales, coinify at 5× sell price, loot boxes, market), cowbells (premium: 80 from the tutorial, rare loot boxes, real-money purchase as tradable "Bag of 10 Cowbells", or bought for coins on the market — opened cowbells are untradable), task tokens, labyrinth tokens, guild tokens/credits/points, dungeon keys. Market: order book with listings up to 30 days, trades only inside ±10 % of an hourly market value (wider for new items), 4 % seller tax (18 % on cowbell bags), price limits that walk with the range; taxes feed community buffs; the developer publishes hourly prices/volume as a public JSON API. Sinks: enhancing (consumes gear copies/protection items/essences/coins), alchemy coin costs, production inputs (median 37–2 511 coins of inputs per XP depending on skill), house room upgrades, labyrinth/guild upgrades, task rerolls, cowbell spending (convenience upgrades: queue slots, offline hours, market listings, loadouts, task slots; cosmetics: chat icons, name colours, avatars; community buffs; 500-cowbell name change), MooPass (subscription: XP buff on standard characters, +offline hours, +market listings, +queue, +task slots/rerolls, labyrinth path length, loot log, golden border). Real-money items are convertible to coins via the market (cowbell bags), so whales fund F2P players' cosmetics/convenience — but no purchasable item adds combat or skilling power directly beyond the MooPass XP buff and community buffs (which benefit everyone).
