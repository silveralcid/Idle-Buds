## Model results (`simulations/mwi_model.py`)
- **XP curve**: L100 → L125 costs 9× the XP of 1→100 (90 M vs 10 M; cumulative XP at 125 is 10× that at 100); cumulative XP at 200 is 1 000× that at 125. A gathering skill with no gear (best unlocked action, level efficiency only) reaches 50 in 18 h, 80 in 227 h, 100 in 1 136 h, 125 in 9 466 h, 200 in 6.3 M h — the top of the curve is deliberately unreachable without stacking speed, efficiency, wisdom and community buffs, and works as a leaderboard axis.
- **Production** XP is input-bound, not time-bound: median input cost per XP at the snapshot's average price (else top ask, else top bid) is 37 coins (cooking), 103 (brewing), 660 (tailoring), 946 (cheesesmithing), 2 511 (crafting); items with no ask fall back to the top bid; cooking and brewing mostly sell above input cost, crafting/cheesesmithing/tailoring mostly below — XP is bought with coins earned elsewhere.
- **Enhancing** at enhancing level = item level, no tool, no protection: +5 in 115 attempts, +10 in 19 465, +15 in 8.0 M, +20 in 3.3 B; protection from +2 cuts +10 to 1 069 attempts + 330 protection items and +20 to 4.2 M attempts + 1.3 M protections. Being 50 levels under the item multiplies +10 attempts ~25× with protection from +2 (26 482 vs 1 069; 14.7× unprotected); 50 levels over cuts attempts only ~22 % (the over-level success bonus is 0.05 %/level) but also gives +50 % action speed (`calcEnhancingActionSpeedBuff`), so time falls about 48 %.
- **Hit chance** has an exponent of 1.4: 2× the target's evasion gives 72.5 %, 3× 82.3 %, half gives 27.5 %.
- **Tiers**: a T5 monster has levels 2.25 × (lvl + 100), defense 1.75 × (lvl + 100), XP 3.5 × (xp + 25) and drop rate 1.5 × (rate + 5·perTier) capped at 1.
