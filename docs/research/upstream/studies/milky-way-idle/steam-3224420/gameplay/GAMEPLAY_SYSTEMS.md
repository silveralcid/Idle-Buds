# Gameplay systems
## Core loop (VERIFIED by data + guide)
One character performs one action at a time (queueable), online or offline (10 h offline cap by default, extendable via Cowbell Store/MooPass): gather (milking, foraging, woodcutting) → process (cheesesmithing, crafting, tailoring, cooking, brewing) → upgrade (alchemy, enhancing) → fight (7 combat skills) → trade everything on a player market. 17 skill tracks (plus total level), 760 actions, 957 items.
## Skilling (values from community data maps, STRONG INFERENCE; key sets VERIFIED against the bundle)
Gathering: milking 7, woodcutting 7 and foraging 33 actions share one ladder — levels 1, 10, 20, 35, 50, 65, 80 with base times 6, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 30 s and 5, 7.5, 12.5, 20, 30, 40, 55 XP (foraging has 4–5 spots per rung); each has an essence drop and rare drops (meteorite caches, branch of insight…). Production: cheesesmithing 205, crafting 198, tailoring 143, brewing 70, cooking 31 recipes, levels 1–106, 6–135 s; 38 "★" refined-equipment upgrades (tailoring 20, cheesesmithing 12, crafting 6) at levels 100–106 consuming refinement shards (enchanted, pirate, labyrinth…). Each level above the requirement adds 1 % efficiency; tools (8 per skill) add action speed; teas/coffees (64 drinks) add efficiency, gathering, wisdom, processing, gourmet, artisan; decimal input quantities are rounded up with a chance to use one less.
## Enhancing & alchemy (VERIFIED formulas, see FORMULAS #4–8)
Enhancing (+1…+20) adds 2 %…100 % of the item's positive base combat stats (to 2× at +20; ×5 for back, charm, earrings, neck, ring and trinket → up to 6×); not enhanced: attack interval, negative penalties (e.g. magic accuracy on 127 items, auto-attack damage on 31), weapon special stats (blaze, bloom, curse, fury, mayhem, parry, pierce, ripple, weaken) and pouch food/drink slots; failure resets to +0 unless protected (copies of the item, mirrors of protection, or components). Alchemy: coinify (5× sell price, 70 % base), decompose (60 %), transmute (item-specific, catalysts), unrefine.
## Combat (VERIFIED structure; resolution server-side)
59 combat actions in 12 categories (11 zones + dungeons) (55 with difficulty tiers T0–T5, 4 dungeons with T0–T2), 95 monsters (10 labyrinth, 8 guild). Five styles (stab, slash, smash, ranged, magic), four damage types (physical, water, nature, fire), 57 abilities (56 learnable from books, 9 of them special; `/abilities/promote` has no book); 5 ability slots from `abilitySlotsLevelRequirementList` = [0, 1, 1, 20, 50, 90], indexed by slot number with index 0 unused: slot 1 is the special-ability slot (Int 1, l.38806), slot 2 the first regular slot (Int 1), slots 3–5 at Int 20/50/90, consumables with configurable combat triggers (54 condition types in the data map; the bundle names 55, adding `boss_status`), threat-weighted targeting, enrage timers per monster (180 s for the 44 regular zone monsters; 600 s for 10 zone bosses, 23 dungeon-only, 10 labyrinth and 8 guild monsters), party size per zone (`maxPartySize`: 44 zones solo, 11 zones up to 3, dungeons up to 5) with a level-gap XP penalty, respawn timers.
## Meta systems
House rooms (17, 8 levels each: the 7 combat rooms give +1 skill level per level, the 9 skilling rooms +1.5 % efficiency per level, the Observatory +1 % action speed and +0.05 % enhancing success per level; every room also gives +0.05 % wisdom and +0.2 % rare find per level), achievements (77 in 6 tiers with tier buffs), collection & bestiary points, tasks (task board every 8 h → 4 h with upgrades, 8 slots, rerolls doubling), Labyrinth (unlocks at total level 1 000; floors of skilling/combat rooms with its own shop/tokens), guilds (trials weekly: 4 skilling + 2 combat; buildings, shrines, guild credits, 10 guild buffs), community buffs (bought with cowbells, server-wide, up to level 20, also fed by market taxes), leaderboards.
