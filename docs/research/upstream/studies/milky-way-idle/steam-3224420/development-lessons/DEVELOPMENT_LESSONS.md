# Development lessons
Adopt: (1) **server-authoritative idle** with data pushed at login (`init_client_data`) — one source of truth, hot-tunable balance, offline progress computed server-side; (2) **document your formulas in-game** — it creates a theorycrafting community and simulators for free; (3) **public market API + official mod/userscript support** in the desktop wrapper; (4) **level-above-requirement efficiency** (+1 %/level) keeps old content useful and smooths the curve; (5) **difficulty tiers** (T0–T5 with a closed-form scale) multiply combat content ×6 without new art; (6) **tax → community buff** loop turns a money sink into a shared reward.
Avoid: (1) a 10⁴× cumulative-XP span between level 100 and 200 invites bots and burnout; (2) enhancement reset-to-zero with 30 % late success rates produces catastrophic variance (the FAQ has to explain that ordinary failures are never compensated, and a paid 500 M reversal exists for confirmed losses); (3) shipping a 2 MB main chunk with every news post, patch note and the English strings inline (the other locales are lazy-loaded chunks, l.3151–3160, which is the pattern to copy); (4) relying on the community to reverse-engineer server combat (the simulator is the de-facto spec).
